# AI crawlers are named and allowed on purpose.
#
# Two different jobs hide behind one word. A TRAINING crawler takes the text
# into a model; a RETRIEVAL crawler fetches the page when somebody asks a
# question and cites it back. We want the second, and the second is the one
# that sends people here — so the retrieval agents are allowed explicitly
# rather than left to a default that a CDN or a future policy change might
# turn into a block.
#
# Everything published here is written to be quoted: primary sources, no
# invented figures, and no client identifiable in any of it.
