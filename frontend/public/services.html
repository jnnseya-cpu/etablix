The cheapest place to fix a project is before it exists.

Our pre-construction group turns concepts into decisions: feasibility studies, conceptual estimates, value engineering, logistics planning and phasing strategy. Estimates carry stated accuracy bands — ±10% at concept, ±5% at schematic, ±3% at design development — and we stand behind them.

  • Value engineering that adds valueAlternatives priced with lifecycle cost, not just first cost. Average client saving: 6–9% of budget.
  • Long-lead risk mappingSwitchgear, elevators, curtain wall — procurement risk identified and bought out early.
  • Site logistics & phasingCrane strategy, laydown, traffic and occupied-facility phasing modeled before mobilization.

Common Questions

Straight answers, before you even ask.

What project sizes do you take on?
Our sweet spot is $5M to $150M in constructed value. Below that we selectively take fit-out and rollout programs where our systems still pay for themselves; above that we partner or joint-venture.
Do we have to use Construx and Veryx on our project?
Yes — and it costs you nothing. Every ETABLIX project runs on our platforms because that's how we hit our numbers. You get owner-level access from day one: live schedule, live budget, live quality scores.
How do you price pre-construction?
Flat fee, scoped by milestone deliverables. If we go on to build the project, we credit the pre-con fee against construction. If we don't, you keep every deliverable — estimates, schedules, risk registers — with no strings.
What does your change-order process look like?
Every potential change is logged in Construx with cost and schedule impact before work proceeds, and you approve digitally. Our change-order rate is under 2.5% of contract value because our pre-construction closes gaps before they're priced as surprises.
Can you work in occupied and regulated facilities?
It's a specialty. Our crews hold ICRA and occupied-facility certifications, and Veryx provides continuous environmental monitoring — noise, dust, vibration — with alert thresholds you set with us before mobilization.
