Cookies

The cookies and similar technologies used on this website — deliberately few, and only what the site needs to operate.

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026 · This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on this website.

1. Our approach

This website is deliberately light on tracking. We do not run advertising cookies, cross-site trackers or third-party analytics on the public website. We use only what is needed to operate the site securely and, in the employee area, to keep an authenticated session working.

2. What we use

Name / typePurposeCategoryDuration
Session storage (employee portal) Holds the signed session token after an employee signs in to the internal workspace, so the dashboard can authenticate API requests. Strictly necessary Cleared when the browser tab session ends or on sign-out; tokens expire automatically after 12 hours.
Server logs Standard request logs (IP address, time, path) used for security monitoring and fault diagnosis. Strictly necessary Up to 12 months.
Google Fonts The website loads the Barlow Condensed, Archivo and Inter typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests font files from Google's servers; Google may record the technical request. Functional (third-party resource) Per Google's own policies.

3. What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No third-party analytics on the public website.
  • No cookie banners demanding consent for things we do not do.

4. Managing cookies and storage

You can clear or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary storage will prevent the employee portal from keeping you signed in, but does not affect the public website.

5. Changes and contact

If we introduce any new cookie or tracking technology, this page will be updated first, and consent will be requested where the law requires it. Questions: contact@etablix.com.
