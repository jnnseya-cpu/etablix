Modern slavery

Our statement under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015: the steps ETABLIX takes to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking in its business and supply chains.

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026 · This statement is made pursuant to section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and sets out the steps ETABLIX takes to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking in its business and supply chains.

1. Our commitment

ETABLIX, part of Groupe Nseya, has zero tolerance for modern slavery, forced or compulsory labour, human trafficking and child labour — in our own business and anywhere in our supply chain. We expect competent work, lawful employment and fair treatment on every site we manage. This is a stated operating commitment of the company, not an aspiration.

2. Our business and supply chains

We provide integrated site services — planning, procuring, integrating and controlling temporary-site and workforce-accommodation services — across the UK, Ireland, Europe and selected international programmes. Our supply chain includes specialists in temporary and enabling civil works, temporary MEP and building services, cleaning and soft FM, modular and accommodation, security and access, logistics and transport, catering and living services, and environmental and closeout works.

Several of these categories — cleaning, catering, security, accommodation operations and logistics — are recognised as higher-risk sectors for labour exploitation. Because we integrate and manage these services directly, we treat labour-practice assurance as a core management responsibility, not a paperwork exercise.

3. Policies and standards

  • Our Supplier Code requires lawful employment, right-to-work checks, lawful wages, working-time compliance and fair treatment, and prohibits forced and child labour throughout the supply chain.
  • Supplier registration requires confirmation that competence, HSE, quality, insurance and financial records can be produced on request.
  • Engagement terms give us rights of audit, investigation and termination for labour-practice breaches.

4. Due diligence

  • Prequalification: suppliers are assessed against project-specific capacity, competence, safety, quality, insurance and commercial requirements before engagement — including labour-practice indicators in higher-risk categories.
  • Mobilisation gates: workforce documentation and welfare arrangements are checked before services begin, not after.
  • Site presence: our integration role puts our people in daily contact with supplier workforces — accommodation, catering, cleaning and security teams included — which gives practical visibility of working conditions that desk-based audits miss.
  • Records: supplier performance, incidents and concerns are recorded in our management systems with named responsibility for closure.

5. Risk indicators we act on

  • Workers without control of their own identity documents or bank accounts.
  • Accommodation tied to employment on exploitative terms.
  • Recruitment fees charged to workers, or undisclosed sub-tier labour supply.
  • Hours, deductions or conditions inconsistent with lawful employment.

Any indicator triggers investigation. Substantiated findings lead to protection of the affected workers first, then corrective action, supplier suspension or removal, and referral to the appropriate authorities where a crime may have been committed.

6. Training and responsibility

Responsibility for this statement and its operation sits with the Founder & Managing Director. Personnel with procurement, site-management and supplier-integration roles are briefed on the risk indicators above and on the escalation route for concerns.

7. Raising a concern

Anyone — including workers on sites we manage and supplier personnel — can raise a modern-slavery concern in confidence at contact@etablix.com. In an emergency, or where someone is in immediate danger, contact the police on 999. The Modern Slavery & Exploitation Helpline is available on 08000 121 700. We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

8. Review

This statement is reviewed and republished at least annually, and whenever our operations or supply chain change materially.
