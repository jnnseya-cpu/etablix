Sectors

Eight sectors. One delivery discipline.

Commercial & Office

Ground-up towers, corporate campuses and workplace transformations — often around live tenancies where every after-hours shift and freight-elevator slot is choreographed in Construx.

  • Core & shell, spec suites, tenant improvements
  • Occupied-building refits with tenant-comfort monitoring
  • Typical range: $5M – $120M

Industrial & Logistics

Distribution hubs, cold storage, and light manufacturing where the business case is measured in speed-to-rack. Tilt-up expertise, early steel buyout, and floor flatness verified by Veryx — FF/FL numbers on record, not on trust.

  • Tilt-up & PEMB structures, ESFR-ready shells
  • Cold chain & food-grade environments
  • Typical range: $10M – $150M

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Hospitals, ambulatory centers and labs — inside facilities that never stop caring for patients. ICRA-certified crews, negative-pressure containment, and Veryx environmental sensors reporting to your infection-control team in real time.

  • Live-campus renovations & expansions
  • Imaging, OR and cleanroom environments
  • Medical gas, isolation and pressure regimes verified

Education & Institutional

Schools, universities and civic buildings delivered inside summer windows and public budgets — with the documentation discipline public procurement demands, generated automatically instead of assembled frantically.

  • Summer-slam renovations, phased occupied campuses
  • Bond-program reporting & audit-ready records
  • Prevailing-wage & compliance administration

Multi-Unit Residential

Mid- and high-rise residential where profit lives in the floor cycle. Repeatable trade sequencing, unit-by-unit Veryx inspections, and a closeout file that accelerates occupancy sign-off and unit handovers.

  • Podium, mid-rise and tower typologies
  • Unit-level quality scoring & punch analytics
  • Typical range: $15M – $100M

Retail & Hospitality

Flagships, hotels and multi-site rollout programs. Opening dates are marketing commitments — we treat them as contractual ones. Rollouts run as a production line in Construx: one playbook, many sites, visible portfolio status.

  • Flagship fit-outs & brand prototypes
  • Multi-site rollout programs (10–200 locations)
  • Hotel refurbishment, wing-by-wing, guests in-house

Public Sector & Infrastructure

Government buildings and public works with airtight compliance: certified payroll, DBE tracking, and a tamper-evident record of every inspection and test. Auditors love us. Genuinely.

  • Municipal & agency facilities
  • Transit-adjacent and utility structures
  • Full public-procurement documentation trail

Energy & Utilities

Substations, plant buildings and utility infrastructure under the strictest safety and access regimes. Permit-to-work discipline, continuous monitoring, and an EMR of 0.42 that lets us work where others can't bid.

  • Substation & switchyard buildings
  • Plant structures & equipment foundations
  • Energized-adjacent work protocols
