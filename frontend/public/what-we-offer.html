Home / What we offer

One accountable system for the services that enable the project.

ETABLIX gives owners, EPCs and major contractors one route to define, procure, mobilise and control the site services that would otherwise consume multiple internal teams and supplier relationships.

Discuss your requirement
01The commercial proposition

Keep your leadership focused on the asset. We integrate the site environment that enables it.

Your project team should be driving design, construction, commissioning and commercial outcomes — not resolving gaps between cabins, utilities, roads, cleaners, security, transport, catering and accommodation providers.

ETABLIX becomes the integration layer between your project requirements and the specialist supply chain. You retain the contractual control appropriate to your risk appetite; we provide the structure, coordination and visibility needed to make the agreed site-services system work.

02Clear scope boundary

We enable the core project. We do not replace its main contractor.

ETABLIX's accountability is defined around the temporary site environment and workforce-support system. The permanent asset remains with the client, EPC or appointed main-works contractor unless a separate, explicit appointment says otherwise.

ETABLIX integrates

  • Temporary site infrastructure and compounds
  • Enabling site civils and reinstatement
  • Temporary MEP and building services
  • Welfare and workforce accommodation
  • Cleaning, facilities and living services
  • Security, access, logistics and transport
  • Site-service procurement and supplier controls

Core project responsibility remains with

  • Permanent asset design
  • Main civil and structural construction
  • Permanent process and production systems
  • Permanent MEP installation
  • Core-asset testing and commissioning
  • Statutory designer or main-contractor duties
  • The client, EPC or appointed main-works contractor

04The managed desk model

Aggregation without transferring uncontrolled cashflow risk.

The desk can give the client one operational interface and, where agreed, one invoice. The commercial structure is selected to preserve continuity and keep supplier funding transparent.

Preferred

Fee-based management

Client contracts or pays suppliers directly. ETABLIX charges a defined management fee for specification, procurement, coordination, control and reporting.

Protected

Back-to-back aggregation

ETABLIX consolidates the scope or invoice only where client payment timing, supplier terms and credit exposure are contractually aligned.

Optional

Performance and savings

A baseline can support a share of verified savings from off-hire control, duplicated services, avoided extensions and recovered commercial leakage.

Working-capital rules

  • No unfunded supplier float
  • Mobilisation payment before commitments
  • Client funds received before supplier exposure
  • Back-to-back payment and suspension rights
  • Credit, liability and project-value gates

07Value across the delivery cycle

We add control before problems become project events.

ETABLIX creates value at every stage by connecting temporary site operations, support-service packages, commercial controls and supplier interfaces to the asset delivery plan.

01

Concept & feasibility

Translate workforce, location, access, constraints and operating assumptions into an early site-services strategy.

Value added

Avoid underestimating the space, utilities, welfare, logistics and accommodation needed to make the project executable.

02

Design development

Coordinate temporary and enabling civil works, temporary MEP, temporary works, compounds, access, village and operational requirements as one system.

Value added

Reduce design gaps, spatial conflicts and late interface decisions between separately designed packages.

03

Procurement

Create package scopes, requirements, evaluation criteria, interface schedules and commercial qualifications.

Value added

Improve bid comparability, expose exclusions earlier and procure suppliers against the complete operating requirement.

04

Mobilisation

Control design release, permits, temporary works, supplier readiness, logistics, commissioning and first-use gates.

Value added

Protect the project start by proving that interconnected services can operate safely — not merely that individual suppliers have arrived.

05

Construction & operations

Run one management rhythm for services, KPIs, incidents, cost, change, programme, evidence and escalation.

Value added

Keep the core construction team focused while daily site systems remain coordinated, measured and accountable.

06

Commissioning & transition

Coordinate temporary-service continuity, interfaces with permanent systems, workforce reduction, defects and handover.

Value added

Prevent the transition between construction, commissioning and operations from creating service gaps or duplicated cost.

Demobilisation & closeout
