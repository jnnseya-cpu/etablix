02Clear scope boundary

We enable the core project. We do not replace its main contractor.

ETABLIX's accountability is defined around the temporary site environment and workforce-support system. The permanent asset remains with the client, EPC or appointed main-works contractor unless a separate, explicit appointment says otherwise.

04The managed desk model

Aggregation without transferring uncontrolled cashflow risk.

The desk can give the client one operational interface and, where agreed, one invoice. The commercial structure is selected to preserve continuity and keep supplier funding transparent.

Preferred

Fee-based management

Client contracts or pays suppliers directly. ETABLIX charges a defined management fee for specification, procurement, coordination, control and reporting.

Protected

Back-to-back aggregation

ETABLIX consolidates the scope or invoice only where client payment timing, supplier terms and credit exposure are contractually aligned.

Optional

Performance and savings

A baseline can support a share of verified savings from off-hire control, duplicated services, avoided extensions and recovered commercial leakage.

Working-capital rules

  • No unfunded supplier float
  • Mobilisation payment before commitments
  • Client funds received before supplier exposure
  • Back-to-back payment and suspension rights
  • Credit, liability and project-value gates

Workforce-linked demand model

Service capacity follows the workforce curve · Illustrative
Welfare, accommodation, catering, transport and utilities are stepped against the trade-by-trade workforce curve — avoiding paid-for overcapacity and productivity-killing undercapacity alike.

Off-hire & idle-hire control

Where the managed desk recovers leaked cost · Illustrative
A single desk that owns off-hire dates, removals and demobilisation closes the gap between falling demand and running hire cost — money projects routinely leak.
