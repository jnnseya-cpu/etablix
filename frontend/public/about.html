Our ETABLIX

Experienced leadership. Integrated delivery. Clear accountability.

ETABLIX Site Systems was created to solve a recurring infrastructure problem: critical site services are often divided across many packages, yet no single party manages the complete operating system.

Our Purpose

Make complex sites easier to mobilise, operate and close.

We combine project leadership, construction management, procurement, supplier integration and digital controls to manage the interfaces between temporary and enabling civil works, temporary MEP and building services, utilities, accommodation, logistics, security, cleaning, facilities services and reinstatement.

The name

ETABLIX is coined from the French établir — to establish, to set up — the exact verb used on every francophone construction site for site establishment (établissement du chantier). It is the business in one word: ETABLIX establishes, runs and removes the entire temporary world around a construction project. Bilingual by design, it reads naturally in English and French, matching the group's Birmingham–Kinshasa footprint — and it follows the Groupe Nseya naming system (CONSTRUX, VERYX): a hard, technical "-X" suffix that signals a systems company, not a labour-hire firm.

Positioning statement

ETABLIX plans, procures, integrates and controls the temporary infrastructure and workforce-living systems that keep major projects operational. Clients obtain one accountable interface from initial requirements and mobilisation through daily operation, demobilisation and reinstatement — controlled through CONSTRUX and led by senior construction professionals.

Mobilised correctly · Operated visibly · Removed completely

What we are — and what we are not

We do not position ourselves as the replacement main contractor for the permanent asset. We take responsibility for the defined site-services system surrounding and enabling those main works. Specifically, ETABLIX is:

  • Not a generic project-management consultancy
  • Not merely a modular-building or camp supplier
  • Not an employment agency or labour-only intermediary
  • Not a facilities-management company detached from construction programme and commercial controls
  • Not a principal contractor by default — the contractual role is explicitly selected for each engagement

How our role grows

Our role can begin as an adviser defining the requirements, develop into a management-integrator appointment, or — where the commercial and risk conditions are appropriate — become a single integrated site-services contract.

We sell accountability and integration — not simply the individual services that the supply chain already provides. Fragmented site-service packages become one controlled scope, one commercial model, one delivery baseline and one management system.

How We Operate

Four commitments behind every appointment.

Accountability

Ownership is defined. Decisions, interfaces, actions and evidence have named responsibility.

Commercial discipline

Scopes, change, payment, supplier performance and forecast outcomes are controlled transparently.

Safe, ethical delivery

We expect competent work, lawful employment, fair treatment and robust health, safety and environmental performance.

Useful technology

CONSTRUX and VERYX support judgement with evidence, controls and earlier visibility — not technology for its own sake.

Founder & Managing Director

Justin Ngolu Nseya, MCIOB

Founded by a Chartered Construction Manager with senior delivery and subcontract-management experience across European grid, offshore wind, rail and public infrastructure, ETABLIX was created to solve a recurring major-project problem: too many critical site services, too many interfaces and no single accountable operating system.

Justin brings senior project, construction, subcontract and commercial-management experience across grid infrastructure, offshore wind, transport and major public programmes. His professional background includes Construction Subcontract Manager assignments with GE Vernova Grid Solutions across the UK, Ireland, Northern and Southern Europe; the 1,400 MW Sofia Offshore Wind connection programme; National Grid infrastructure projects; Programme Delivery Manager at the West Midlands Combined Authority; Senior Project Manager at Mott MacDonald across rail, education, justice and public infrastructure; and Project Manager at Turner & Townsend in PMO, controls, procurement support, scheduling and delivery governance.

He holds an MSc in BIM Management, a BSc in Construction Management, Chartered Membership of the CIOB, APMP and PRINCE2 — with working fluency in Primavera P6, CEMAR, Revit, Navisworks and common data environments, connecting site systems to programme, commercial administration and BIM-enabled controls.

Professional experience is provided as background and does not imply endorsement by former employers or clients.
