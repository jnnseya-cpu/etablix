# Three months of runway: what actually pays the mortgage

## The arithmetic, said once and plainly

**ETABLIX will not pay a mortgage within three months.** Not because the
proposition is wrong — the GCA just confirmed in writing that the diagnostic is
a qualifying contract example, which is real validation — but because of the
cycle. A new company with no completed contract, no insurance in place and no
certification sells its first advisory engagement in two to six months from
first contact, and a single small diagnostic does not cover three months of a
household.

Pretending otherwise would be the most expensive thing in this document. So the
plan below has two tracks, and **the first one is not ETABLIX.**

## Track A — the thing that actually solves it, and it solves two problems at once

**Contract back into the transmission market, through JNN GLOBAL LTD.**

The market is live and it is named. In April 2026 National Grid appointed
thirteen contractors to a dynamic framework for a substation works surge. SSEN
Transmission is letting 400 kV substation work under the £10 billion Accelerated
Strategic Transmission Investment framework — BAM and Siemens Energy took Greens
in Aberdeenshire. The top tier for EPC substation construction is BAM Nuttall,
Costain, Laing O'Rourke, Siemens Energy and Skanska, all competing for turnkey
schemes on live 400 kV sites.

Every one of those firms is staffing up right now for work they have just won.
And the profile they cannot find enough of is **exactly** the one on the CV:
chartered construction manager, HV substation and overhead line delivery,
subcontract management, Primavera P6, NEC, CEMAR, three years inside GE Vernova
Grid Solutions on precisely this type of scheme, across the UK and Ireland.

That is a day-rate market and it moves in weeks, not months. Two or three
agency conversations will establish the current rate far better than any guess
here.

### And here is the part that matters most

**If the engagement is contracted to JNN GLOBAL LTD rather than to you
personally, it is a contract performed by the bidding entity.**

Re-read what the GCA confirmed: a contract example is valid where the
organisation acted as **prime contractor, sub-contractor, or a member of a Group
of Economic Operators.** There is no minimum value. A completed project phase
counts, where all deliverables for that phase are finished. It must fall within
the technical service filters selected, and construction project management and
subcontract management sit squarely inside Construction Professional Services.

So a contract role, structured correctly, produces:

- **Income inside weeks**, which is the actual problem.
- **The completed contract example** that every single one of the twelve stalled
  procurement threads is waiting for.
- **A customer who can sign Attachment 1a**, and who has just watched you
  deliver.

One decision closes the cash gap and unlocks the framework. Nothing else
available does both.

### The three conditions, and they are not optional

1. **It must be a business-to-business contract with JNN GLOBAL LTD as the
   supplier.** An umbrella arrangement or agency PAYE is employment. It pays the
   mortgage and it produces nothing for the DPS. Push for outside-IR35 or
   direct-to-client engagements for this reason, and say so to the agencies — it
   is a normal thing for a limited company contractor to insist on.
2. **Ask about the confirmation of works at the start, not the end.** Put it in
   the engagement letter: on completion of each defined phase, the client will be
   asked to countersign a short confirmation of the services delivered. Asked at
   scoping it is a formality. Asked six weeks after the final invoice it is a
   favour.
3. **Check with the GCA who may sign** where the contract is through an agency —
   the agency is your customer, but confirm whether Attachment 1a may be signed
   by the agency or must be signed by the end client. One email, asked before
   the engagement rather than after it.

## Track B — the fastest realistic ETABLIX money

Run this alongside, in the evenings. Three rules:

**Warm only.** Twelve procurement threads produced no revenue. Three live
referral routes exist and former colleagues at Mott MacDonald, Turner & Townsend,
the West Midlands Combined Authority and GE Vernova are the warmest market
available. Cold outreach has a six-week lead time to a decision and cannot land
inside the window.

**Small enough to avoid procurement.** The reason a first sale stalls is not
price, it is the approval route. Most organisations let a manager approve
low-value professional services on a purchase order without a tender. Price the
first engagement to sit under that threshold deliberately. A small invoice paid
this quarter is worth more than a large proposal in a procurement queue, and the
Attachment 1a is identical either way.

**Approach as a supplier, never as a candidate.** The AtkinsRéalis notice is the
lesson: a capability email carrying a curriculum vitae reads as an agency touting
a person, and the answer is a supplier-code notice against the whole company.
Describe what the company does. Never attach the CV.

## The conversation to have this week, before anything else

**Telephone the mortgage lender now, while the account is up to date.** This is
the single highest-value hour in the whole plan and it costs nothing.

A lender dealing with a customer who is in good standing and says "my income
stopped, here is my plan, I need breathing space" has real options available,
and is regulated to treat customers in financial difficulty fairly. The usual
ones are a payment deferral, a temporary switch to interest only, or extending
the term to reduce the monthly payment. All of them are easier to arrange
**before** a payment is missed. After arrears begin, the same conversation is
harder and the credit consequences are already running.

**Get free regulated advice rather than taking any of this as financial
advice.** StepChange, National Debtline and Citizens Advice are free,
confidential, and deal with exactly this situation every day. They will also
check whether there is any entitlement being left unclaimed, which after ten
months may be material.

## What to stop doing

**Stop applying to frameworks.** Every one of them is blocked on the same thing,
and no further submission changes that. The documents are built. They will be
waiting when there is an Attachment 1a to attach.

**Spend nothing on Cyber Essentials or insurance until there is income.** They
are prerequisites for appointment, not for registration, and the frameworks are
open for four years. They can wait three months. Cash cannot.

**Do not build anything else.** The platform, the sixteen agents, the policy set,
the specimen and the bid documents are all finished to a standard well beyond
what the current stage requires. More building is the most comfortable way to
avoid selling, and it is the thing that has filled ten months.

## The honest summary

The business is not the problem. The sequence was. Ten months went into building
something genuinely good and into twelve applications that all terminate at the
same locked door, and none of it could ever have produced cash in the window
available.

**The way out is to go back into the market you know, through your own company,
and let that engagement be both the income and the contract example.** Then
ETABLIX has a signed Attachment 1a, a referee who has just watched you work, and
a founder who is not three months from the wall.
