## The profile everything is assessed against

accounts, no revenue. Birmingham. Construction consultancy plus two software

## Tier 1 — apply now, no eligibility problem, no cost

### 1. Innovate UK Business Growth (formerly Innovate UK EDGE)

The best fit on this list, and the one to do first.

| | |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | UK-registered trading company, up to 250 employees. No revenue threshold in the published criteria |
| Cost | Free |
| What you get | A named adviser, growth and innovation support, and access to a **£15,000 grant to spend with Catapults and Research and Technology Organisations** |

**Why it matters more than it looks.** That £15,000 voucher is spent *with a
Catapult*. It is the legitimate side door into exactly the ecosystem the
Infrastructure Productivity programme sits inside — an adviser, a relationship
and a funded piece of work with the organisation whose scale-up programme you
could not qualify for. It reaches the same place from the stage you are
actually at.

**Action:** contact Innovate UK Business Growth and ask to be assigned an
adviser. Expect a conversation, not a form.

### 2. Business Growth West Midlands

| | |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | SME based in Birmingham, Coventry, Dudley, Sandwell, Solihull, Walsall or Wolverhampton. Birmingham B44 qualifies |
| Cost | Free, government funded through the UK Shared Prosperity Fund |
| What you get | Free hands-on expert advice through Birmingham City Council and the WMCA-coordinated service |

**On the grant arm, be clear-eyed.** The West Midlands SME Grant Programme —
which ran £2,500 to £7,500 for Birmingham start-ups and £5,000 to £100,000 for
existing SMEs — has been **fully committed and closed to new applications since
20 August 2025.** The Business Energy Advice Service grant closed to new
applications after 7 January 2026. The advice service is live; the money is
not.

**Action:** register with Business Growth West Midlands for the advice, and
email `wmgp@birmingham.gov.uk` to register interest in future grant rounds.
Ten minutes, and it costs nothing to be on a list when the next round opens.

## Tier 2 — genuinely available, but read the warning first

### 4. Start Up Loans — British Business Bank

| | |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Businesses trading under **five years** (raised from three on 6 April 2026), so this company qualifies |
| Amount | £500 to £25,000 |
| Rate | 7.5% fixed, repaid over 1 to 5 years |
| Extra | **12 months of free mentoring**, which is worth having on its own |

> **This is a personal loan, not a company loan.** It is unsecured and lent to
> the director personally. You are liable for it whatever happens to the
> company, and it appears on your personal credit file.
>
> That matters here more than usual. The DPS assessor has already raised a
> guarantee, and a personal loan is the same exposure arriving by a different
> door. Take it only for something that produces revenue — insurance, SSIP,
> accreditation, a first hire — and never to fund time.

The mentoring is available to successful applicants only, so it is not a
reason to borrow on its own.

### 5. R&D tax relief — worth one conversation with the accountant

Not an application. Money back for work already done, which beats every grant
on this page if it applies.

| Scheme | Applies to | Worth |
|---|---|---|
| Merged R&D scheme | Accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024 | A 20% taxable credit, roughly 14.7p to 16.2p per £1 of qualifying spend |
| ERIS — Enhanced R&D Intensive Support | Loss-making SMEs where qualifying R&D is at least 30% of total expenditure | Up to about 26.97p per £1 |

ETABLIX is loss-making, and if platform development is most of what it spends,
the 30% intensity test is plausibly met.

**Three honest cautions, because this is where companies get hurt.**

- **Most software work does not qualify.** The test is an advance in science or
  technology and the resolution of technological uncertainty. Building an
  application with established technologies, however hard the work, generally
  fails it.
- **HMRC has been aggressive on software claims** and the enquiry rate is high.
  A rejected claim costs the fee, the tax and a penalty.
- **A claim needs qualifying expenditure.** If the founder drew no salary and
  did the work himself, there may be very little to claim against however good
  the argument is.

**Action:** one conversation with the accountant, described honestly. Do not
engage a contingent-fee R&D claim firm that approaches you — that sector is
where the bad claims come from.

## Tier 3 — possible but do not start this month

### 6. Innovate UK Smart Grants

Always open, with quarterly rounds. Any UK-registered business with an
innovative project can apply, and there is no turnover threshold. Feasibility
studies from around £25,000 to £100,000; industrial research up to £500,000 for
a single applicant.

**Why it is tier 3 and not tier 1.** Success rates are low, the application is
a serious piece of work measured in weeks, and it needs a genuine technical
advance rather than a good product. Those weeks currently belong to the CHIC
submission and the RM6242 financial assessment, both of which this company can
actually win. Revisit when there is a real technical question worth funding and
the applications are away.

## The order to do this in

1. **UK Export Academy** — sign up. Minutes, free, no eligibility question.
2. **Business Growth West Midlands** — register, and email Birmingham City
   Council to be on the list for the next grant round.
3. **Innovate UK Business Growth** — request an adviser. This is the one with
   the £15,000 Catapult voucher behind it and the longest payoff.
4. **The accountant**, on R&D relief. One conversation, honestly framed.
5. **Start Up Loans** — only if there is a specific purchase that produces
   revenue, and only with the personal liability understood.

Items one to three cost under an hour in total and none of them can be
refused on eligibility.

## Two standing rules that apply to all of it

**Never pay to apply, and never pay to be listed.** Every programme on this
page is free. Anything of this shape asking for a fee is a different kind of
thing.

**Never claim what is not held.** Grant and programme applications are audited,
and a company that overstates its position on one becomes a company with a
record on all of them.
