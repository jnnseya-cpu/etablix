# National Grid · TBM Caroline breakthrough · Grain to Tilbury, 2.2 km under the Thames

**The post.** Tunnel boring machine Caroline completes a 2.2 km drive beneath the
Thames on the Grain to Tilbury project, **49 days ahead of schedule**. Credits
Ferrovial, BEMO Tunnelling and Herrenknecht. Part of The Great Grid Upgrade.

**The angle is the 49 days, and nobody else will take it there.** Finishing a
phase seven weeks early is a win on the works and frequently a dead cost on the
establishment, because accommodation, welfare, parking, security and catering are
contracted against the baseline programme and carry minimum terms and notice
periods of their own. Beating the programme only converts into money if the
temporary estate can follow it.

That is an observation about contract structure, not about anybody's competence,
so it congratulates without any edge. And it goes straight at the part of our
scope that is hardest to explain in the abstract — the terms, not the cabins.

**Different again from the previous three.** Azizi was travel time as paid time;
Kier was the temporary/permanent split; Balfour was accommodation as a capacity
constraint. This one is early completion as an unrecovered cost. Four posts, four
distinct ideas, all from the same scope.
