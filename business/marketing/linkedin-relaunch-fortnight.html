The launch ritual — run it before every post

  1. T-24h: DM 8–10 people the comment-ask. Fresh names each post; repeat only proven engagers.
  2. T-0: Publish 8–10am Tue/Thu, personal profile, native image attached, zero links in the body.
  3. T+1 min: Post the link yourself as the first comment.
  4. T+0–90 min: Stay online. Reply to every comment within minutes — replies count as new engagement and trigger wider distribution.
  5. Same day: Personal DM to anyone from a target company who engaged. End of day: log impressions.

The two messages

Comment-ask · to people who know you · T-24h "I'm posting something about the business tomorrow morning — the first hour decides whether LinkedIn shows it to anyone. A comment (even one line) would genuinely help me get it seen. I'll return the favour."
Connection request · to buyers, directors, suppliers · 5–8/day "Building a UK integrated site-services business — one accountable partner for the temporary infrastructure on major programmes. Your work on [project/company] is close to what we do; keen to follow it."
