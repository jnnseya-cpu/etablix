## What they actually are

Founded 2015. Structural and geotechnical design. Water and wastewater
(reservoirs, treatment tanks, pumping stations, a diaphragm-walled water intake
at over 11 m excavation), port infrastructure (a concrete quay, an 18.64 m clear
span steel walkway, a mobile construction platform supporting staged pile driving
and drilling), road infrastructure (a 15 m steel-concrete composite bridge), and
an industrial steel building of 5,393 m² at 16 m clear height with overhead
cranes and runway beams. Allplan, SAP2000, Tekla Structures. Two directors, both
civil engineers with postgraduate structural qualifications — Giovana on steel,
Felipe on concrete, steel, foundations and strengthening.

**The part that matters.** Page 14 of their portfolio states their own operating
model: design support inside the client's project team, with the locally
authorised engineering team retaining responsibility for local review, approval
and sealing. They have not asked to be the designer of record. That is the
correct posture and it is the only reason this is a real conversation rather than
a polite refusal.

## Why it is written this way

**The nothing-live sentence goes near the top, not the bottom.** He has asked a
direct question and he is a director, not a salesperson. Burying the honest
answer under the pleasant part of the reply is how a register loses its
credibility before anybody has registered on it.

**Answering his page fourteen back to him does the qualifying work for free.**
He has already accepted that the locally authorised team signs. Quoting his own
model back, with the UK specifics attached — UK National Annexes, BS 5975, a
design check category, PI responding in this jurisdiction — tells him exactly
where the line is without a single word of negotiation. It also makes clear that
his portfolio was read, which almost no reply to a cold introduction manages.

**Naming the temporary works is the one piece of real commercial insight in the
letter.** Their own portfolio leads on reservoirs and treatment tanks. The
staged pile-driving platform and the diaphragm-walled excavation are buried on
pages eight and eleven, and they are the items closest to what ETABLIX buys. A
reply that re-sorts somebody's portfolio for them is remembered.

**The five numbered points are the answer to the actual question.** The question
was how two companies in non-overlapping markets use each other. The answer is:
a register entry, a capability note each way, a named-with-consent referral in
both directions, nothing exclusive, no money, no NDA yet, and one call a year.
That is small enough to actually survive, which is the only property that
matters in a standing arrangement nobody is being paid to maintain.

**What the reply deliberately withholds.** No work is offered and no pipeline is
implied. No client, scheme or former employer is named. No accreditation is
claimed — ETABLIX holds none to claim. No price. Nothing implies a Principal
Designer or Principal Contractor appointment under CDM 2015, and the designer
duties are named as sitting on the designer of record precisely to keep that
clear. And no NDA is signed as an opening courtesy.

## Before anything becomes real

If a package ever does arise for them, three things get checked before the
enquiry goes out, not after: that their professional indemnity cover responds in
England and Wales, that a UK designer of record is appointed and has accepted the
design check category in writing, and that the split of responsibility between
their analysis and that designer's approval is recorded in the subcontract rather
than in an email thread. The arrangement is only as good as those three lines.
