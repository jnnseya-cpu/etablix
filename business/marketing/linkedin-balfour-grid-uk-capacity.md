# Balfour Beatty · GRID UK roundtable · "Tripling investment: the expertise and capacity challenge"

**The post.** Three themes from a Balfour Beatty roundtable at GRID UK: plan
capability beyond individual projects; enable long-term investment through
regulation; turn collaboration into collective action. Closing line: the
challenge is building national capability, not meeting the next project's
demands.

**The best match of any post so far, because the subject is capacity.** A
roundtable on skills, regulation and supply chain capacity will not have
discussed the physical limit on capacity: whether the people recruited can be
housed within reach of the work. Grid reinforcement happens in the Highlands,
mid-Wales, the East Anglian coast and Teesside. Training and recruitment do not
create capacity at a 400 kV site if there are no beds within commuting distance
of it. The accommodation estate is a capacity constraint and it is almost never
in the capacity conversation.

**And theme one is the company's founding observation, in their own words.**
"Plan capability beyond individual projects" is precisely what nobody does with
the temporary estate: it is specified, built, run and removed scheme by scheme,
then reassembled close to fresh forty miles away for the next one. That
observation across a portfolio of new-build sites is why ETABLIX exists. It can
be said here without stretching, because they raised it first.

## The change: first person

The last two comments said "we specify at ETABLIX" and "our director ran". He is
commenting from his own profile and he **is** the director, so corporate voice
reads as somebody writing on his behalf. First person is more natural, more
honest, and performs better on a personal profile. From here on, personal-profile
comments are written in the first person; company-page posts keep the corporate
voice.
