## The judgement first

This is a celebration post. It is not a sales opportunity and treating it as
one is the fastest way to be remembered badly by somebody whose good opinion
is worth more than any single job.

But it is a legitimate reason to be visible, and the thing that makes a
comment visible is not warmth — it is specificity that only one person could
have written. Justin worked there. That is the asset.

**One comment. No pitch. No link. No "which is why we…".**

## What Turner & Townsend actually is to ETABLIX

Worth being clear-eyed, because it is both things at once.

**A competitor, partly.** T&T sells programme management, cost management and
procurement advisory. Model 01 Advisory — strategy, technical requirements,
procurement documents, evaluation — is in the same category of purchase, on a
much narrower subject. Nobody at ETABLIX should pretend otherwise.

**A channel, mostly.** T&T advises the clients who mobilise the sites. It does
not procure, integrate or operate the temporary site establishment, and it
does not want to: there is no fee in it for a firm of their shape, and it sits
below the level they are engaged at. A cost manager on a large programme who
has a site-services package nobody has scoped properly has a problem ETABLIX
solves and they do not.

**So the approach, when there is one, is never "we do what you do, cheaper".**
It is "here is the one package on your programme that nobody has defined, and
we will define it under your programme's rules". That is an entirely different
conversation and it does not compete with anybody's fee.

**Not now, though.** Not under this post.
