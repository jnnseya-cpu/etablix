# LinkedIn comment — Craig Little, Advance Grid Solutions, two BESS appointments

**Post, 14 October 2026.** Craig Little, Managing Director at Advance Grid
Solutions, announces two grid-scale battery storage appointments totalling
100 MW and more than 300 MWh, at roughly three hours' duration. Most of the
post is credit given away: site teams and engineers for delivering safely and
to a high standard, commercial and business development for the relationships
and the winning bids, and — unusually — the office and support staff who "keep
everything moving behind the scenes."

## The comment

> The line about your office and support staff is the one I would keep.
>
> On two concurrent sites the administration does not double, it more than
> doubles — every supplier at every compound is a separate order, a separate
> invoice query and a separate performance conversation, and the people you
> have just thanked are the ones who absorb all of it.
>
> That interface load is exactly what ETABLIX takes off a delivery team. Well
> done to all of them, and congratulations Craig.

*82 words, three paragraphs, one idea.*

## Why this one

**Everybody else will comment on the megawatts.** 100 MW and 300 MWh are the
numbers on the graphic, and a hundred replies will repeat them back. The post
is not really about the megawatts. Craig spent four sentences giving the credit
to his people and one of them to the back office, which almost no managing
director bothers to write.

**So the comment starts where his post ended, not where his graphic started.**
Picking up the line most commenters will scroll past is the whole technique
here: it proves the post was read rather than reacted to, and it is the part he
chose to write himself.

**And that line is also, honestly, where ETABLIX lives.** The administration of
multiple site-service suppliers is precisely the load that falls on office and
support staff, and on two simultaneous compounds it scales worse than linearly
because nothing can be shared between them. That is a real observation about
two concurrent remote sites, not a pivot.

**It is peer to peer.** Managing director to managing director, first name at
the end, and the compliment is to his team rather than to him — which is what
he asked for in the post.

**What it withholds.** No price. No invented statistic — the scaling point is
stated as a structural fact, not quantified, because no number exists that can
be stood behind. No client, scheme or former employer named. No accreditation.
Nothing implying a CDM 2015 duty holder appointment. No exclamation mark, and
nothing suggesting anybody is doing anything badly: the argument is that the
load is real and lands on people who did not choose it.
