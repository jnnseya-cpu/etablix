# LinkedIn comment — Bilfinger UK, Hinkley Point C order

**Post, 14 October 2026.** Bilfinger UK announces a multi-million-euro order
intake for EDF (UK)'s Hinkley Point C, running to 2030: detailed engineering,
procurement, piping prefabrication and installation. Around 300 specialists on
site now, up to 800 at peak, plus a further 100 at their prefabrication
facility in West Carr.

## The comment

> The number I keep returning to is the 100 at West Carr. Every operative
> prefabricating off site is one who needs no bed, no seat on a bus and no
> place in the canteen at Hinkley — and the on-site peak is what sets the whole
> site establishment bill.
>
> Going from 300 to 800 is a step change in welfare, transport and
> accommodation that gets committed years before the peak arrives, not at it.
>
> At ETABLIX that trade-off is the first thing I model. Congratulations to the
> team.

*85 words, three paragraphs, one idea.*

## Why this one

**The West Carr number is the one nobody else will comment on.** Every other
reply will take the 800. The interesting figure is the 100, because off-site
prefabrication is the only lever that reduces a peak workforce rather than
accommodating it — and the on-site peak is what prices the welfare, the
transport, the beds and the catering. Reading their own announcement back to
them through that lens says more about what ETABLIX does than any description
would.

**It praises the right decision rather than diagnosing a problem.** West Carr
is the clever part of their answer and the comment says so. Nothing suggests
anybody is doing anything badly, which is the rule, and here it is not even a
constraint — the observation is genuinely admiring.

**The commitment timing is the sell, stated as a fact about programmes rather
than a pitch.** A jump from 300 to 800 is ordered years ahead of the peak it
serves. That is precisely the window where the work is worth doing and the
reason it so often is not.

**What it withholds.** No price. No invented statistic — the 300, the 800 and
the 100 are theirs, quoted as published. No former employer and no scheme of
mine is named. No accreditation. Nothing implying a CDM 2015 duty holder
appointment. No exclamation mark, and nothing that reads as a question with an
obvious answer.
