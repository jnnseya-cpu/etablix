# Leah Terry · Berkswell BESS, 200 MW / 857 MWh, near Solihull

**The post.** Leadership search consultant in infrastructure and energy, on BW
ESS signing a ten-year optimisation agreement with Danske Commodities for the
full Berkswell project. Construction began September 2026, operational Q4 2027.

**The angle, and it is specific to battery storage.** A BESS is almost entirely
temporary. Once it is energised it is effectively unmanned — so unlike a
substation or a plant with permanent operational facilities, the compound,
welfare, parking and access exist only for the build, and **all of it comes out
again.** There is no "it will be used in operation" argument to soften the
removal cost. On a battery site, reinstatement is the whole story.

**Also local.** Berkswell is a few miles from the registered office. A West
Midlands note lands warmly with a West Midlands audience and costs one clause.

**A recruiter, not a buyer** — so the offer stays implicit. Leadership search in
infrastructure and energy sees who is building what before most people do, which
is worth more here than a direct ask.
