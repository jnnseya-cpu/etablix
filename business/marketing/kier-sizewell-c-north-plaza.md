# Kier Infrastructure — Sizewell C, North Plaza

**The post (two weeks old):** Kier Infrastructure has started on site at
Sizewell C, initial works underway on the North Plaza. A 15-hectare plaza
providing bus interchanges, car park, bus park, HGV stacking area and
personnel security facilities, plus the main access road running through it.
Kier cites its Hinkley Point C experience. 35,560 followers on the page.

## What this post actually tells us

**The North Plaza is a site establishment.** Not adjacent to our subject, our
subject exactly: access, parking, HGV management, security screening, and the
welfare that goes with them. Somebody is buying every one of those systems on
this programme, at scale, for years.

**Hinkley Point C is the precedent, and it is a published one.** The Hinkley
model separated the licensed nuclear island from the things around it —
park-and-ride, campus accommodation, freight management. Sizewell C is
following the same shape, and the published consent documents describe the
workforce accommodation and associated infrastructure. Anybody in this market
should read those consent documents before approaching anyone; they are
public and they name the programme's own requirements.

**Numbers to check, never to quote from memory.** Peak workforce, campus bed
numbers and programme dates for Sizewell C are all in the public consent
documentation. Look them up and cite the document. Do not repeat a figure
from a news article, and never from this note.

## Where ETABLIX could actually sit

**Not on the licensed nuclear site, and not soon.** Work inside a nuclear
licensed site carries a qualification burden — nuclear site licence
conditions, the regulator's expectations of the licensee's supply chain,
nuclear-specific quality regimes — that a business of this size does not
clear on a first engagement, and pretending otherwise on a prequalification
form is how a supplier gets permanently marked.

**The realistic target is everything OFF the licensed site.** Park-and-ride,
freight management facilities, campus accommodation, the plaza itself, and the
welfare and utilities serving all of them. Same programme, same client
hierarchy, a fraction of the qualification barrier — and it is the majority
of the temporary estate by area.

**The route in, in order:**

1. **Sizewell C's own supply chain registration.** The programme runs a
   supply chain engagement process and registration is free. Register there
   before approaching any Tier 1, because the first question a Tier 1 asks is
   whether you are already in the programme's system.
2. **Kier's supplier registration**, also free, as a separate track.
3. **Then a specific conversation about a specific package**, never a
   capability introduction. "We do site establishment" is a brochure. "Your
   campus welfare and utilities package needs defining before it is let, and
   here is a specimen of what that document looks like" is a conversation.

**Never pay for any of it.** No paid portal, no paid directory, no paid
accreditation body dressed up as a prequalification requirement. A genuine
programme registration is free; anything asking for money to be listed is
selling a list.

## What must not be said to anybody at Kier or Sizewell C

- **No claim of nuclear experience.** We have none.
- **No implication that ETABLIX could hold CDM 2015 Principal Contractor**
  on a nuclear programme. It could hold it nowhere by accident and here not
  at all without an explicit, priced, insured appointment that nobody is
  going to offer a first-time supplier on this project.
- **No numbers about Sizewell C from memory.** Cite the consent documents or
  say nothing.
- **Never that a main contractor does this badly.** Kier has done it at
  Hinkley. The argument is capacity and attention, and on a programme this
  size the capacity argument makes itself.
