# LinkedIn post: asking subcontractors to register

**The constraint that shapes it: there are no live packages.** A recruitment post
implying otherwise is the same lie by implication as a dishonest email, except
broadcast. So the post has to be attractive *because* it is honest, not despite
it.

**The hook does that work.** Most subcontractor registers waste a supplier's
time, because nobody says precisely what they buy. Saying it precisely — and
saying "if you build the structure we are not your client" — is a courtesy the
reader will not have had from anyone else that week. It also filters, which is
the point of a register.

**And the honest reason to register now is a real one:** a package arrives with a
short runway, and the prequalified suppliers are the ones who get the enquiry.
Nobody builds a supply chain in the week they need it.

First person, per the standing rule. On the company page, change "I would rather
say" to "we would rather say" and leave the rest.

## The post

> Most supply chain registrations waste a subcontractor's time, because nobody
> tells you precisely what they buy.
>
> So, precisely: ETABLIX is not a main contractor and does not tender permanent
> works. If you build the structure, we are not your client. If you establish,
> service, operate or remove the site around it, we are.
>
> Six categories, twenty-one trades:
>
> → Design and temporary works engineering
> → Establish the site — earthworks, bases, temporary roads, drainage, fencing,
> traffic management
> → Utilities — power, water, wastewater, temporary MEP, connectivity
> → Buildings — modular, worker accommodation, plant and furniture
> → Operate — security, catering, cleaning, waste, medical, logistics, workforce
> transport
> → Demobilise — removal, off-hire, reinstatement
>
> Where we actually are: no live packages today. Our engagements are in
> procurement, not on site. I would rather say that than have anyone register on
> the strength of a maybe.
>
> Register anyway, and here is the honest reason. When a package comes it comes
> with a short runway, and the suppliers already prequalified are the ones who
> get the enquiry. Nobody builds a supply chain in the week they need it.
>
> Defined packages, not labour-only. Bring your insurances, accreditations and a
> capability statement.
>
> https://etablix.com/subcontractors#register

## Notes

**The first two lines carry it.** Everything after "see more" is read only by
people the hook already caught, so the honesty paragraph sits safely in the
middle and the link at the end.

**Two things deliberately left out.** Which commercial model applies and
therefore who pays the subcontractor — true and attractive, but it is detail for
the page and the follow-up email, not for a post. And any suggestion of pipeline,
scale or client names, because there are none to give.

**Expect few registrations and the right ones.** A post that says "no work today"
will convert worse than one that implies otherwise and far better than one that
is found out.
