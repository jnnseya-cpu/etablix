# Farhad Azizi · Burj Azizi × KONE · 725 m, Dubai

**The post.** Group CEO of Azizi Developments announces KONE for the elevators
and escalators on Burj Azizi. Two themes: that a 725 m tower makes every detail
matter "including how people move through it", and that a family name behind a
business brings personal responsibility and a long-term outlook.

**The angle, and why it is not a stretch.** The post is about how people move
through the *finished* tower. The thing nobody posts about is how they move
through it *while it is being built*. On a supertall, temporary vertical
transportation is a programme driver: thousands of operatives, a fixed number of
hoists, and travel time that is paid but appears in no rate. That is adjacent to
his subject rather than a swerve away from it, and it runs straight into our
scope — the same arithmetic horizontally is an accommodation village that is not
connected to the work it serves.

**The family-name line is a genuine hook, not flattery.** He said he values it.
JNN GLOBAL LTD is a family name behind this business. One sentence, no more.

## Two deliberate omissions

**The scheme and the employer are not named.** Skye and SSEN Transmission are
public, and GE Vernova employment is on the LinkedIn profile already — but a
public comment is not the place to publish a former employer's project structure
while an MNDA with that employer is sitting unsigned on the desk. The 309
bedrooms and the five packages carry the weight without the names. Add them only
once the NDA position is settled, and never the commercial terms.

**No offer of help on Burj Azizi.** A one-director UK company suggesting it could
take the workforce scope on a 725 m Dubai supertall would read as the comment of
somebody who has never seen a project that size. The comment states a principle
and leaves the door where it belongs.

## Checks

No price. No claim that anyone does this badly — the argument is that the cost is
unpriced, never that the people pricing it are not good enough. No invented
figure: 309 bedrooms is confirmed against the project record. No CDM implication.
The director's work is attributed to the director, not to ETABLIX.
