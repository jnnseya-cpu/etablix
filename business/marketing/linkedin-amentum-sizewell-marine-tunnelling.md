# LinkedIn comment — Amentum, Sizewell C marine and tunnelling design

**Post, 14 October 2026.** Amentum UK and International selected by the Civil
Works Alliance to lead design of marine and tunnelling works at Sizewell C.
Estimated value £22.3 million to Amentum: a three-year design project followed
by seven years of design support. Subcontractors Jacobs on major tunnelling
design support and ADDA on 3D reinforced concrete detailing. Scope covers three
3.5 km offshore tunnels, marine shafts and tunnel-to-shaft connections, marine
heads, onshore galleries, connections to the main civil works, and a fish
return system. CWA comprises Balfour Beatty, Bouygues Travaux Publics, Laing
O'Rourke and Sizewell C Ltd. Loren Jones is quoted on replicating and enhancing
lessons learned from Hinkley Point C, where Amentum led equivalent scope.

## Why this one

**Everybody else will comment on the tunnels or the cooling water.** Three
3.5 km offshore bores and 4,200 Olympic pools a day are the arresting numbers,
and they are also the numbers with nothing of mine to add. The structural fact
in that announcement is the contract shape: three years of design, then seven
years of support. Ten years of the same designer.

**That shape is the strongest version of ETABLIX's own argument, made by
somebody else.** Continuity fails in this industry for a mechanical reason
rather than a cultural one — the people who learned the lesson have gone before
the lesson is needed. Amentum's contract answers it by keeping the designer
contractually present for the seven years after the drawings. Site
establishment has exactly the same failure and almost never the same answer: it
is set up by whoever is free in the fortnight before mobilisation and then
handed on, so nobody who made the decisions is there when the consequences
arrive.

**It praises a decision rather than diagnosing a fault.** The observation is
about how a contract was written, and it is admiring. Nothing suggests any
party does this badly, and the general remark about demobilisation is about
timing, which is structural and true of everyone.

**What it withholds.** No price — the £22.3 million is theirs, quoted as
published, and nothing of ETABLIX's is mentioned. No invented statistic. No
former employer and no scheme of mine named. No accreditation. Nothing
implying a CDM 2015 duty holder appointment. No exclamation mark, and no
question with an obvious answer.
