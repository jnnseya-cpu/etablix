# Kier Infrastructure · Sizewell Link Road · 6.8 km, A12 to Sizewell C

**The post.** Kier Infrastructure awarded the Sizewell Link Road under the Major
Civils Framework: 6.8 km connecting the A12 to the construction site, to carry
construction traffic, cut HGV movements through local communities, and improve
local journey times. Craig Small FICE MCIOB, Managing Director for Nuclear &
Energy, quoted.

**Why this post and not the Sizewell C account.** The Suffolk Chamber portal
refused us on 30 September for want of a permanent East of England base. **Kier
has no such requirement.** It is a tier-one contractor with a national supply
chain and a growing Sizewell C role, and it is reachable. Commenting here is the
better route to the same project, and the offer is therefore aimed at Kier's
supply chain rather than at Sizewell C.

**The angle.** A link road is construction logistics infrastructure that happens
to be permanent. Everything else in that same chain — compounds, parking,
welfare, accommodation, and the bus movements the road is sized for — is
temporary, and temporary is where an estimate goes soft. It credits the work,
says something nobody else in that thread will say, and lands in our scope
without a swerve.

**Deliberately a different line from the Azizi comment.** That one was about
travel time as paid time. Running the same observation twice in a week looks
like a man with one idea. This one is about the temporary/permanent split and
where pricing discipline stops.
