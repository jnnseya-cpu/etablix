# Marketing

`linkedin-week-one.html` — **ETABLIX Launch Week.** The first week on LinkedIn:
profile and company page setup, the opening posts, and what to say before
there is any delivery record to point at.

`linkedin-relaunch-fortnight.html` — **The Relaunch Fortnight.** The two-week
run: day by day, with the post copy, the reason each post exists and what it is
trying to produce.

`followup-week-two.html` — **The Follow-Up Sequence.** Three messages to three
former colleagues who did not reply the first time, written from the work that
was actually shared with them rather than from a supplier introduction.

`cold-introduction-500.html` — **The 500-Contractor Introduction.** The cold
outreach to the main-contractor address list: a short forwardable email for the
switchboard and recruitment inboxes that hold most of those addresses, the
longer Model 01 · Advisory pitch for the named person a redirection produces,
one follow-up, the legal footer, and the sending rules — starting with the DKIM
keys, because 500 messages from a domain whose signatures fail is how a young
domain gets classified as a spam source for good.

These were written in working sessions and lived only in an ephemeral scratchpad
until they were asked for again and found to be one container restart away from
gone. They are in the repository now for that reason. Open any of them in a browser.
