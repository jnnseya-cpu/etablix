# Berwick Bank B — Siemens Energy preferred supplier, 2,600 UK jobs

**The post.** SSE plc with Siemens Energy, 21 hours old, 236 reactions, 6
comments, 26 reposts. Siemens Energy selected as preferred supplier for the
wind turbines and substations at Berwick Bank B. Headline figure 2,600 UK jobs:
more than 2,200 existing and project-based roles through Siemens Energy wind
and grid technology operations in Hull, Newcastle and Manchester, and more than
450 engineering, project delivery and servicing roles with SSE and Siemens
Energy across Scotland. Framed against COP31 Türkiye, welcomed by the Prime
Minister and the Energy Secretary.

## The angle

**The 2,600 is a manufacturing and operations number.** Read the breakdown the
post itself gives: factory and grid technology operations in three English
cities, and engineering, project delivery and servicing roles in Scotland.
Those are people in existing buildings, on payroll, mostly where they already
are.

**The headcount that sizes a construction compound is a different number.** An
offshore wind farm's onshore construction scope — landfall, cable route,
onshore substation, and the marshalling port during installation — carries its
own peak, in a different place, on a different date, and it does not appear in
a preferred-supplier announcement because at that stage nobody has had to work
it out.

**Why it is a new argument.** Every previous comment was about a construction
site and the people on it. This one is about the gap between the number a
company announces and the number a compound is sized against — which only
arises where the announcement is a supply-chain jobs figure rather than a site
one. It could not have been written for any of the others.

**Why it is not carping.** It does not say the figure is wrong or inflated. It
says what the figure counts, which the post states itself, and then names the
scope it does not cover. "Not settled at preferred-supplier stage" is an
observation about the stage, not about anybody's competence.
