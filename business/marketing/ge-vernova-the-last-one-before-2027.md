# GE Vernova — the last one before 2027, and it asks for nothing

The decision in `ge-vernova-stop-and-reroute.md` stands: closed until 2027.
This is one email to the **construction and sourcing function** — not another
approach to Marc, Martin or Mark, who already had their closing lines and
should not get a second.

What makes it consistent with those lines rather than a contradiction of them
is that it asks for nothing at all. No meeting, no form, no reply, no
registration, no introduction. It shows the sample and it leaves.

## One thing to check before sending

A six-month restriction running from a February leaving date expired in
August. It is now September, so on the face of it the period has run. That is
an observation about dates and not legal advice — if the contract carried a
longer restriction, a different trigger date, or a non-solicitation clause
worded separately from the non-compete, read it before sending. An email that
asks for nothing is the lowest-risk form of contact available, but "low risk"
is not the same as "checked".

## What must not be in it

- **Nothing may suggest ETABLIX delivered anything for GE Vernova.** The
  turnkey work was Justin's, as their employee. Saying "some of you will know
  me from the turnkey projects" is true and safe. Anything that reads as a
  credential for the company is not.
- No reference to any private exchange with anyone, at GE Vernova or
  elsewhere. Not one.
- No pricing. This is not a proposal.
- No mention of who has or has not replied to earlier emails. Nobody is owed
  an explanation and raising it makes the email about the sender.

## Why it is shaped this way

- **The no-ask is stated twice, early and late, and it is the whole point.**
  An email from a former employee that wants something is one of twenty in the
  inbox. An email that explicitly wants nothing is unusual enough to get read
  to the end, and it is the only form that does not contradict the closing
  lines already sent.
- **"I left in February and set up ETABLIX" does the credential work without
  making a claim.** They can place him in a second. Nothing is asserted about
  what the company has done.
- **One insight, not a capability list.** The Section 278 paragraph is
  concrete, it is a failure they have all seen, and it demonstrates the method
  instead of describing it. Everything else was cut to make room for it.
- **"File it or bin it"** is the line that makes the no-ask credible. Offering
  somebody permission to ignore you is the opposite of pressure, and it is
  what a person who is not desperate says.
- **The 2027 sentence closes the loop deliberately.** It names the return
  date, so the silence between now and then is a plan rather than a
  disappearance — and it means the next approach arrives as something promised
  rather than something remembered.
- **The subject line carries the no-ask.** It has to survive being read in a
  preview pane by somebody deciding whether to open it.
