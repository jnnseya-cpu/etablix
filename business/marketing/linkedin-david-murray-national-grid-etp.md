for a comment on his post is the tier-1 contractors he sells to.

## Where the addition is

The post treats the programme as one thing: substations, overhead lines and
associated infrastructure. From a site establishment point of view those are
two different problems, and the overhead line half is the one that gets
under-provided for.

- A **substation** is one compound, held for two years or more. Establish it
  once, amortise it over the whole job.
- **A thousand kilometres of overhead line** is dozens of short-duration
  compounds that move along a route. Access tracks, laydown, stringing sites,
  welfare, security, reinstatement — on land held under wayleave or temporary
  agreement rather than possession, with a different landowner and a different
  set of constraints every few kilometres.

Establishment cost per pound of construction value runs materially higher on
linear work, and it recurs with every move instead of being spent once. Most
preliminaries models are built around a fixed compound, because most of the
experience behind them is.

The second point is his own language. He is a major accounts manager, so supply
constraint is what he thinks about all day: £35bn to 2031 across three ETP
partners means they draw on the same regional welfare, plant and accommodation
pool at the same time. That bites on availability before it bites on price.

## Why it is shaped this way

- **"Most of us grew up with" is the whole safety mechanism.** The observation
  is that prelims models were built for fixed compounds. Phrased as a shared
  industry inheritance it is a structural point; phrased as "contractors price
  this wrong" it would be an accusation against three companies David sells to,
  and he would not thank anyone for it. Never say a contractor does the work
  badly. Capacity and structure are safe ground; competence is not.
- **The domain is named openly here, unlike on a CEO's post.** David is a
  supply chain peer writing a pipeline post. "Site establishment" is the
  correct technical term for what the comment is about, and using it costs
  nothing.
- **The availability point is written for him specifically.** Three partners
  drawing on one regional pool is an account manager's problem, stated in an
  account manager's terms. It is the line most likely to get a reply.
- **No figures added.** The £1.2bn, the 1,000km and the £35bn are all his.
- **No pitch, no company named, no ask.** The last two sentences are the
  positioning and the four paragraphs above them earn it.
- **"Held by agreement rather than possession"** is the phrase that proves the
  comment was written by someone who has actually done linear works. It is also
  simply accurate.
