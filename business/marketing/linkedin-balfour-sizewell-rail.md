# LinkedIn — Balfour Beatty, first freight train at Sizewell C

**The post:** Balfour Beatty resharing Sizewell C's announcement that the first
commercial freight train has arrived on the new rail route, and crediting their
teams and partners with bringing it to life. Photographs of the locomotive at
night and of the people who built it.

**Why this one is squarely ours.** A rail head at a nuclear new build is
enabling infrastructure. It exists to serve construction logistics and to keep
lorries off rural roads — it is not part of what the station will be remembered
for. That is exactly the scope this company is formed around, so the comment can
be entirely genuine rather than reached for.

## Why it works

**The last line is the comment.** "A milestone rather than a footnote" praises
the post itself and makes an argument at the same time. Enabling works are
routinely treated as overhead, and a tier-one choosing to celebrate one is worth
naming approvingly. It will be read as generous because it is.

**"Worked backwards from hardest" is the practitioner's tell.** A rail
connection needs consents, land, a network connection and a possession strategy,
all reverse-engineered from a construction programme years before the first
train. Anybody who has done that recognises the phrase; anybody who has not
would never write it.

**The lorry movements line carries the community point without preaching.**
Freight by rail on a rural Suffolk site is a planning and neighbour issue as
much as a logistics one, and naming it shows you understand what the route was
really for.

**Nothing is claimed and nobody is corrected.** It is an appreciation with an
idea inside it, which is the only kind of comment worth leaving under somebody
else's milestone.

## What must not be in it

- **No ETABLIX, no link, no service.** Especially here — the temptation is
  strongest on the post that is closest to what we do.
- **No claim of nuclear or rail experience.** The observation is true of large
  enabling works generally, which is why it stands up. Reaching for Sizewell
  specifically would be the one false note.
- **No accommodation or welfare angle.** It is honest and it is relevant, but
  the post is about logistics and one theme lands harder than two. Save it.
- **No numbers.** Not lorry movements avoided, not tonnage, not workforce.
