# Sweco — the reply, and the list error behind it

**Received, September 2026, from a general enquiries route:**

> Thank you for your enquiry and interest in Sweco. We appreciate the offer,
> but this is not a service we require.
> — Pauline Steggall, Senior Administrator, Sweco UK Ltd, Ipswich

## This is not a rejection of the proposition

Two facts about it, both of which matter more than the word "no".

**Sweco is a design and engineering consultancy.** They do not hold
compounds. They do not set up sites, take possession of land, price
preliminaries, or carry welfare and logistics. So "this is not a service we
require" is not a judgement — it is simply true. The proposition was put in
front of an organisation that has no use for it.

**Pauline is a Senior Administrator.** She was clearing an enquiries inbox,
which is the job. She was not asked to assess whether site establishment
pricing is a real problem in UK construction, she has no reason to have a view
on it, and she did not express one. Reading this as market feedback would be a
mistake; market feedback is what comes back from a commercial manager at a
contractor.

The lesson is a list error, not a positioning error. Nothing about the offer
needs changing on the strength of this.

## The reply to send

Short, gracious, asks for nothing. Administrators who handle enquiry inboxes
see a great deal of pushback, and the one that does not push back is the one
they remember.

> Thank you for coming back to me, Pauline — appreciated, and understood.
>
> That is my error rather than yours. Site establishment sits with whoever
> holds the compound, so it was the wrong thing to put in front of a
> consultancy, and I should have seen that before I sent it. No need to reply.
>
> Kind regards,
> Justin Nseya

Do not ask her to redirect it. A redirect on this proposition leads nowhere,
because no part of Sweco establishes sites. Asking would cost the goodwill of
the graceful exit and buy nothing.

## Where Sweco could matter later — a different proposition entirely

Sweco acts on the client side: project management, Employer's Agent, CDM
advice. Those are the same Category 1 consultant appointments ETABLIX is
applying for on the CHIC DPS.

So Sweco is not a buyer of site establishment. Sweco is a potential **channel**
to buyers of it — because when Sweco is Employer's Agent, *their client* is the
one carrying the establishment cost in the contractor's preliminaries. An EA
who has twice watched a client pay for a compound nobody interrogated has a
reason to want that number examined before it is agreed.

That is a referral conversation with whoever runs their project management
business, on LinkedIn, about their clients' exposure. It is not a supplier
introduction, it does not go to an enquiries inbox, and it does not reference
this thread. Not now — it needs the delivery record first.

## The filter to apply to the remaining list

The cold introduction is written for organisations that **hold the compound and
price the preliminaries**. Sending it to anyone else produces exactly the reply
above, and burns the address in the process.

**Send it to:**

- Main contractors — building and civils, tier 1 and tier 2
- EPC contractors in energy, grid and renewables
- Principal contractors on infrastructure frameworks
- Utility and grid delivery partners
- Specialist contractors that run their own compounds on long-duration sites
  — piling, civils, steel, heavy electrical
- Developers and clients who self-deliver rather than appointing a main
  contractor

**Do not send it to:**

- Design and engineering consultancies
- Architectural practices
- Cost consultants and quantity surveying practices
- Project management and Employer's Agent practices
- Recruitment businesses
- Welfare, cabin and plant hire suppliers — they are supply chain, and on some
  scopes they are the competition
- Software vendors

The test is one question: **does this organisation ever have a site compound in
its own name?** If the answer is no, the email does not go to them, whatever
their turnover and however relevant the sector looks.

The consultancies and the PM practices are not dead ground — they are a
different route, with a different message, sent later. Mixing the two is what
produced this reply.

## What to record

Sweco: closed, correctly, by them. No follow-up on this proposition. Revisit
the consultancy channel after the first delivered engagement, and route it
through project management leadership rather than an enquiries address.
