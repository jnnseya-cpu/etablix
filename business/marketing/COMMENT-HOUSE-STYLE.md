# LinkedIn comments — house style

**Standing rule, from 24 September 2026.** Every comment on a post related to
what ETABLIX does must also sell ETABLIX.

This SUPERSEDES the default in the comment files written before this date,
which all say some version of *"no pitch, no company named, no ask"*. Those
files are kept as written — the comments in them were posted, and the reasoning
in them about accuracy, tone and what not to say still stands. Only the
no-pitch line is overtaken.

Nothing else in this file is new. It is the guardrails that were already being
applied, written down so that adding a commercial close does not quietly drop
them.

---

## The shape

Four parts, in this order. The order is the mechanism: the sell is earned by
what precedes it, and moved to the front it reads as an advert and gets
scrolled past.

1. **Credit.** One line, specific to their post. Not flattery — a sentence that
   shows the post was actually read. "The CCS integration is what makes this
   hard rather than just large" is credit. "Great news!" is not.

2. **The insight.** The longest part. Something true and non-obvious about
   *their* situation that they have not said themselves. This is the whole
   transaction: the reader gives attention, the comment gives something back.
   Without it, parts 3 and 4 have not been paid for.

3. **The bridge.** ONE sentence naming ETABLIX, phrased as the answer to the
   insight rather than as a description of a service. "That tail is what ETABLIX
   does — we price and specify site establishment as one package before
   mobilisation" bridges. "ETABLIX is a site services consultancy offering..."
   does not.

4. **The offer.** Low friction, nothing demanded. The risk reversal, or the
   specimen report, or both. It must be possible to accept with one reply.

## The ratio

**The sell is never the longest part.** Roughly seventy per cent insight, thirty
per cent sell. If parts 3 and 4 together run longer than part 2, the comment has
become an advert and should be cut, not posted. A comment that reads as an
advert is muted, and a muted account cannot sell to anyone.

## What never goes into the sell

- **The price.** Not in a public comment. Two reasons: it reaches competitors,
  and it anchors a tier-one contractor at the single-site wedge before anyone
  has had a conversation. The risk reversal carries the same commercial weight
  with no number attached. Price is quoted out loud in the meeting — that is
  the rule in the go-to-market plan at 8.1 — not published under someone
  else's post.
- **Any claim about how the poster, their client or their partners perform.**
  Time, capacity and structure are safe ground. Competence is not, and never
  becomes so.
- **Invented proof.** No client, no delivered scheme, no statistic, no
  testimonial that does not exist. Until there is delivered work to cite, the
  specimen report IS the proof — and it is described accurately every time:
  an invented project, so no client is identified.
- **Anything implying a CDM 2015 duty-holder role.** "Price and specify" is
  safe. Wording that could be read as Principal Designer or Principal
  Contractor is not.

## When not to sell at all

The rule is "related to what we do". These are not, whatever they mention:

- A fatality, injury, or any safety incident.
- A memorial, retirement or obituary.
- Redundancies, restructuring or a profit warning.
- A personal award, promotion or milestone.

On those, comment with warmth or do not comment. A commercial line under any of
them is remembered for years by exactly the people who matter.

## Two adjustments by audience

- **A supply chain peer rather than a buyer** — someone who sells something
  else into the same contractors. The sell softens to a single line, because
  the relationship is the asset and it is worth more over two years than a
  proposal is this quarter.
- **A corporate page rather than a person.** Comments under a brand account are
  read by the social team. Post it anyway when the post is significant, but
  watch for the named individual sharing it on their own profile within a day
  or two, and put the same comment there. That is the version that reaches
  someone who owns a number on a live scheme.
