## The observation behind it, in full

Gigawatt-scale nuclear absorbs a bespoke site establishment because the
project is enormous and lasts a decade. The establishment is a rounding error
against the reactor, so it gets designed from scratch, once, by whoever is
free.

An SMR programme breaks that. The entire economic argument for small modular
reactors is repeatability: factory-built units, standardised, deployed many
times. If the unit is standardised and the establishment around it is not,
then the establishment becomes a proportionally larger and larger share of
the site cost and schedule with every deployment — and it is re-invented
every time.

That is a definitional argument for a designed, documented, repeatable
temporary-estate system. It is also the argument for advisory work at
programme level rather than project level, which is the higher-value end of
what ETABLIX does.

Worth writing up as a field note eventually. Not yet: it needs the SMR
programme structures to be public enough to cite, and nothing goes in a post
without a primary source.

## The NIA conference on 15 September

**Five days away, in Warrington**, which is the centre of the UK nuclear
supply-chain cluster. So the room is right.

**Check three things before committing anything:**

1. **Is it members-only?** The Nuclear Industry Association runs many events
   for members. If it is, the question becomes NIA membership, which is a
   real annual cost and a separate decision from a conference ticket.
2. **What does it cost, all in?** Ticket, travel to Warrington, and a day.
3. **What is the specific ask?** A small business with no nuclear credential
   attending a supply-chain conference without a specific ask gets a lanyard
   and a coffee. With one specific ask, aimed at two or three named
   organisations, it is worth the day.

**This is a legitimate industry conference, not a pay-to-be-listed scam**, so
the standing rule against paying for directory inclusion does not apply. But
the money test does: it competes with everything else the same spend could
buy, and at this stage free routes are not exhausted.

**The free routes that come first:** Sizewell C's own supply chain
registration, the Great Grid Upgrade delivery partners, and Balfour Beatty's
own supplier registration. All free. Do those before buying a ticket to meet
the same people.
