# LinkedIn — Balfour Beatty plc, safety culture with SSE

**The post:** Balfour Beatty asking how clients and contractors can work
together to strengthen safety culture, linking a Project Safety Journal case
study on their work with SSE across major energy infrastructure in Scotland —
immersive training on how teams decide under pressure, plus collaboration on
planning, risk and wellbeing. 45 reactions, 2 comments.

**Audience:** Balfour Beatty and SSE safety and project people, the Association
for Project Safety, and the wider infrastructure HSE community.

## The rule under a safety post

This is the most dangerous post on the feed to comment badly on. Anything that
reads as "here is what you are missing" under a tier-one's safety initiative
ends the relationship before it starts, and it will be read by people whose job
is safety and who will not forgive being lectured about it by a stranger.

So the comment must be **additive and complementary, never corrective**, must
claim nothing about ETABLIX, and must not apportion responsibility to either
party. What it can do is answer the question they actually asked.

## Why it works

**It answers the question in the post.** They asked how clients and contractors
work together. The answer offered is that some safety-critical conditions are
set at specification stage, before the contractor exists on the job — which is
precisely a client-and-contractor question and not a criticism of either.

**It is complementary, not corrective.** "Training people to decide well under
pressure is worth doing" concedes the point before adding to it. The addition is
that pressure has an upstream source, which nobody in that comment thread will
raise and which is obviously true once said.

**The three examples are ours and they are concrete.** Sleep in daylight, the
minibus, the canteen. They are the same three from the occupational health
policy and the homepage, they are recognisable to anybody who has worked a
remote energy job, and none of them is a statistic.

**It names timing, not a party.** "Before a contractor is appointed" and
"written into a specification months earlier" describe when a decision happens.
Saying "those are client decisions" in a post co-branded with SSE would point at
SSE in their own comment section, which is the one thing that must not happen.

## What must not be in it

- **Nothing about ETABLIX, no link, no service, no CDM role.** A safety post is
  the worst place on the platform to sell, and this company holds no SSIP
  accreditation to stand behind a safety claim.
- **No statistic.** Not fatigue research, not incident rates, nothing. Under a
  safety post, an unsourced number is the fastest way to be corrected publicly
  by somebody who knows the literature.
- **No suggestion that anybody's safety performance is inadequate.** Time,
  conditions and sequencing are safe ground. Competence is not, and never will
  be.
- **No claim that ETABLIX makes sites safe.** The policies written this month
  are explicit that nothing we issue says a site is safe. That discipline
  applies in public too.
