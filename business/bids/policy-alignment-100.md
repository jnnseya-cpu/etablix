## The answer — 99 words

> Yes.
>
> The Building Safety Act 2022 established the golden thread principle —
> accurate, current information held across an asset's life — and section 135

## Why these four

**The Building Safety Act is the strongest and it is stated carefully.** The
answer says the Act "established the golden thread principle" rather than
claiming the statutory duty applies to these projects — the duty attaches to
higher-risk buildings, and most infrastructure is not one. The alignment is
real and the wording does not overclaim it. Section 135's extended limitation
is the same point that drives the retention table in the quality document, so
two documents agree.

**The Construction Playbook is the policy an infrastructure assessor lives
with.** Outcome-based specification and defining interfaces before packages are
let is not an interpretation of it — it is what it asks for, and it is what the
solution does. Naming it correctly is the fastest way to show you work in this
world.

**The AI line is the one nobody else will write.** UK AI policy asks for
explainability and contestability rather than certification. An architecture
where a deterministic engine produces the finding and the model only writes it
up is aligned with that by construction, not by policy statement. "Contestable
against its rule" is the phrase to keep.

**Every line names a mechanism.** Not "we support the golden thread" but
"lineage and bitemporal records are built to that shape". An assessor scoring
this field is looking for the applicant who can connect an instrument to a
feature, and most answers here will be a list of Act names.

## Before you paste it

**Do not claim compliance.** The answer says aligned, not compliant, and that
distinction matters: compliance with the Building Safety Act is a duty holder's
position, not a supplier's product claim. Keep every verb where it is.

**If the form asks for evidence rather than assertion**, the honest addition is
one clause naming where each feature lives — the lineage records, the clause
graph, the engine registry. Only add it if there is room; the answer is
stronger tight than padded.
