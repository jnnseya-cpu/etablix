# "Describe your solution's track record" — 300 words

This is the hardest field on the form and the one where fabrication is most
tempting. **There is no customer track record.** No engagement has been
delivered under the company, so there is no time saved, no cost reduced and no
error rate to quote.

The answer below says that in its first line, gives what genuinely exists,
refuses out loud to manufacture the numbers, and then points at the
demonstration this same application funds as the thing that produces them.

**Nothing from the LinkedIn exchanges appears in it.** Two senior people at
tier-one manufacturers confirmed the diagnosis privately. Those were private
replies given to a former colleague, not endorsements offered for reuse, and
putting them in a funding application would be using them as a sales lever.
They stay out, and the insight stands unattributed as our own framing.

---

## The answer — 300 words

> ETABLIX has no customer track record, and this answer will not invent one.
> Quantitative outcome data for the company does not exist, because no
> engagement has yet been delivered under it.
>
> What exists is in two parts.
>
> **The delivery record is the founder's, and it is quantified.** As
> construction subcontract manager at GE Vernova he carried the civils, site
> management and site infrastructure inside EPC and full turnkey contracts
> across the UK, Ireland and Europe — including a 36-month programme with a
> 309-bed workforce village inside the turnkey scope, where he priced that
> scope, produced the site reports behind it and managed the civils and
> site-services subcontractors delivering it. Earlier, at UK Power Reserve, he
> ran risk and change and then pre-construction across a 628MW programme of
> more than thirty new-build generation and storage sites, each needing access,
> compound, temporary power, welfare and logistics before anything of value
> happened.
>
> **The solution is built, not planned.** Sixteen deterministic engines, the
> clause graph, bitemporal state and the adversarial challenger are implemented
> and under automated test. What has not happened is a client running it on
> their own project. That is the gap, and we state it rather than dress it.
>
> We will not convert the founder's record into productivity percentages for
> this application. Those outcomes were never measured at the time, and a
> figure produced now to fill this box would be an estimate presented as
> evidence.
>
> The demonstration proposed in this application is the answer to this
> question. Three retrospective runs on three organisations' own tender packs,
> marked against outcomes those organisations already know, with the proportion
> of valid findings confirmed by an independent chartered surveyor and reported
> whatever it turns out to be. That produces the quantitative track record this
> field asks for — evidenced rather than asserted, inside the programme window.

## Why this is the strongest answer available

**It cannot be caught out.** Every other answer to this field, for a company
with no customers, either invents numbers or evades the question. This one does
neither, and an assessor who has read thirty applications will have seen a lot
of both.

**The founder's record is quantified and checkable.** 36 months, 309 beds,
628MW, thirty-plus sites. Those are the numbers that make the paragraph
credible, and they are the only numbers in the answer that can be verified.

**The refusal paragraph is the one that earns respect.** Saying out loud that
you will not turn an unmeasured past into a productivity percentage tells an
assessor exactly how you will behave with their evidence later. It is worth
more than the invented figure would have been.

**The last paragraph converts the weakness into the plan.** The form asks for a
track record and offers £8,000 for a demonstration. Connecting the two makes
the application coherent: this is a company that knows precisely what it lacks
and has costed the route to getting it.

## Before you paste it

**Verify the two sets of figures yourself.** 36 months and 309 beds came from
you. The 628MW and thirty-plus sites came from my earlier notes and should be
checked against your own record before it goes on a funded application.

**Confirm the GE Vernova and UK Power Reserve descriptions are yours to make.**
Describing your own role and the shape of the scope is ordinary CV practice.
Contract values, client names beyond the employer, commercial terms and
supplier names are not, and none appear here.

**Do not add a case study.** Not a hypothetical, not an illustrative one, not a
"representative" one. The specimen diagnostic is a fictional project and
belongs nowhere near a field headed track record.
