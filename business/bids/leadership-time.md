# "Leadership time per month" — the answer

A short factual field. Give a number with a breakdown rather than
"as much as it takes", which is not checkable and which every applicant writes.

---

> **Three days per month for scale-up support** — coaching sessions, customer
> days and investor readiness work — in addition to the four days per month of
> demonstration delivery already costed as match funding in the finance table.
>

---

## Why it is written this way

**A number beats a promise.** "Whatever is required" is what most applicants
write and it tells an assessor nothing. Three plus four, totalling seven,
is a commitment somebody can hold you to at a review — which is precisely why
it is worth more.

**Seven days a month is credible for a sole director.** It is substantial
without being implausible. Twelve would be a claim nobody believes from
somebody who also has to earn a living and progress two DPS applications.

**The one-director point is a genuine advantage, stated once.** No approval to
seek and no board diary is true, and it is the one place in this application
where being a company of one is better than being a company of thirty. Say it
plainly and move on.

**The last paragraph is the credibility line.** Committing to flag a conflict
rather than silently miss a session is the kind of thing an assessor has been
let down on before. It costs nothing to promise and it is the promise most
likely to be remembered.

## Before you answer

**Check seven days a month is a number you will actually hold to.** It is the
only figure in this application that somebody will measure you against month by
month rather than once at the end.

**If the programme publishes a required minimum**, make sure your figure clears
it. If it asks for more than seven, say what you can do rather than agreeing to
a number you cannot meet.
