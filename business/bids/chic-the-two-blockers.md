# The two things you think are blockers. Neither is.

## 1. "We have no balance sheet and no cash flow statement"

You have both. What you do not have is **accountant-prepared financial
statements**, and that is a different thing — which CHIC already know, because
they opened by saying *"as you are a start-up company and a formal financial
report is not yet available."*

### A balance sheet is a list, not a report

It is a statement of what the company owns and owes at one date. For a company
at this stage it is one page and perhaps eight lines:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Assets** | Cash at bank. Any money owed to the company. Equipment and computers at cost less depreciation. Prepayments — insurance or software paid in advance. |
| **Liabilities** | Director's loan. Trade creditors. Accruals — the accountant's fee not yet billed. Any tax owed. |
| **Equity** | Share capital. Retained losses to date. |

Every figure comes from the bank statements and the company records. **If it is
small, it is small.** A balance sheet showing modest cash and a director's loan
is a true balance sheet and it satisfies what they asked for. A missing one does
not.

### A cash flow statement is the bank statement, reorganised

Money in and money out for the period to date, grouped: receipts, operating
payments, director's loan movements, equipment purchases. You already have every
number — it is in the bank account.

### So give them three, not one

They said **one or more** of five. Provide:

1. **Statement of financial position** as at [date]
2. **Cash flow for the period to date**
3. **Cash flow forecast with assumptions** — the generator is ready

That clears a bar of one, three times over, **and it retires the guarantor
option entirely.** No bank letter, no delay.

### Label it honestly, and this matters

On the face of every document:

> Management information prepared by the director from the company's own
> records. Unaudited. JNN GLOBAL LTD has not yet reached its first accounting
> reference date.

**Never imply an accountant prepared or reviewed something they did not.** If
your accountant will review the three documents and say so, that is worth
having and worth a small fee. If they will not, submit them as director-prepared
management information, which is exactly what CHIC asked a start-up for.

### On the forecast

Run `node business/bids/build-forecast.cjs`. Fill the shaded cells, and fill the
**Basis** column for every one. They know a forecast from a company with no
trading history cannot be accurate — what they are assessing is whether it is
reasoned. And leave the cash trough in if the model shows one, with a line
saying how it is funded. A stated, funded trough reads as competence.

## 2. "In GE it was Justin Nseya as an employee, not ETABLIX — so we are stuck"

You are not stuck. Read what CHIC asked for again:

> "please provide further details of **your organisation's relevant experience,
> expertise, and delivery capability**. You may also submit client testimonials
> and/or **references, including contact details for referees**"

They did not ask for clients of ETABLIX. They asked for referees. And you are
applying to **Category 1 — Consultants**: Project Manager, Employer's Agent,
Clerk of Works, CDM Co-ordinator.

**Those are personal-competence appointments.** A client appointing an
Employer's Agent appoints a named individual. For a consultancy, the
organisation's capability *is* the named individual's capability — that is not a
workaround, it is how professional-services selection has always worked, and it
is why CHIC's own guide provides for an applicant with no prior contract
examples.

**So a referee does not have to be a client of ETABLIX. A referee has to be
somebody who watched you do the work.**

### Four routes, and the second is the one you have not considered

**a) Former managers.** Your line manager on the 628MW programme ran it and
watched you deliver risk, change and pre-construction across thirty-plus sites.
UK Power Reserve no longer exists, so there is no current employer's commercial
position to protect — which removes the usual reason a senior person declines.

**b) Client-side counterparts on the turnkey projects.** This is the unlock.
The client's project manager, engineer or employer's agent on those jobs **was
not a GE Vernova employee.** They watched you run the civils, site management
and site infrastructure scope from the other side of the table. They have no
conflict-of-interest question, no non-solicitation issue, and they sit entirely
outside the decision to leave your former colleagues alone until 2027. For a
Category 1 consultancy application, a client-side referee is the strongest kind
there is.

**c) Subcontractors you managed.** A civils or site-services contractor who
worked under your packages can speak credibly to how you ran them. They also
have every reason to say yes, because you may be a customer again. Describe them
accurately as supply chain, never as a client.

**d) Earlier employers.** Turner & Townsend and before. Further back, but a
consultancy referee carries weight in a consultancy application.

### On the GE Vernova stand-down

A referee request is not a sales approach. It involves no employer's commercial
position, no procurement, and no business development — it is a personal favour
about what you were like to work with. It does not breach the decision to leave
them alone.

**But the sequencing is now tight.** If you have already sent the "last time
from me, no reply needed" lines, do not follow them with a request three days
later. Go to routes (a), (b) and (c) instead. **If you have not sent them yet,
send the referee request instead of the closing line** — it is a better use of a
final contact and far more likely to be answered.

### What to submit for each referee

Name, job title, organisation, telephone, email, and one line on the
relationship: which project, and what they saw you do. Ask permission first,
every time. A referee surprised by CHIC's call costs you the reference and the
credibility in the same conversation.

**Three referees is the target. Two good ones beat three where one is thin.**
