# Siemens Energy supplier collaboration form — answers, field by field

**Form:** Collaborating with Siemens Energy — supply chain request
**Completed for:** JNN GLOBAL LTD, trading as ETABLIX
**Date:** 15 October 2026

Every answer below is one ETABLIX can stand behind if it is checked. Four
fields on this form are places where a careless answer becomes a false
declaration, and they are called out in their own section. Read that section
before typing anything.

## Where this form sits, and which door it is

The collaboration page offers **two** buttons, and they are not alternatives
for the same thing:

| Button | Goes to | For |
|---|---|---|
| Click here to innovate with us | innovation-orbit.siemens-energy.com/**partners** | An innovative proposal — a technology or solution put forward for assessment |
| Supplier self-nomination (excluding innovation) | innovation-orbit.siemens-energy.com/**supplychainpartners** | Introducing yourself as a new supplier, with no innovation proposal |

**Take the self-nomination.** ETABLIX supplies a service, not a technology
being offered for innovation assessment, and the innovation route puts the
submission in front of people evaluating intellectual property and
co-development rather than buyers of site services. The "Contact us" link
earlier on the same page goes to the same self-nomination address.

**The form already being completed is the right one.** It is served from
innovation-orbit.siemens-energy.com, which is the self-nomination
application. Nothing else needs finding.

**Region.** The page says the region is used to route the nomination to the
correct regional procurement team, so this is the field that decides who reads
it. The legal headquarters is Birmingham, so the answer is the United Kingdom —
**UK and Ireland** where that is offered, **Europe** on the form's own region
list.

### What happens after it is submitted

1. **Self-nomination** — this form. Reviewed for a current or future need.
2. **Registration** — if there is a need, procurement organises onboarding
   through **SAP Ariba**. This is by invitation. There is nothing to be gained
   by going looking for Ariba now; an uninvited account does not advance
   anything.
3. **Qualification modules** — chosen by the service offered. The
   **Sustainability Self-Assessment**, plus modules on **health and safety**
   and **cybersecurity**. These are the real gate, and "Ready-for-Business"
   status is what permits taking part in invitations to tender.

So the form is the easy part. The modules are where this is won or lost, and
two of the three touch a known gap.

### Read three documents before submitting

Submitting commits the company to Siemens Energy's supplier standards, so read
what is being committed to rather than ticking it:

- **Code of Conduct for Siemens Energy Suppliers** — the commitment made at
  registration.
- **Code of Conduct, "Management System for occupational Health and Safety"** —
  the section the health and safety module is assessed against. Worth reading
  closely: it asks for a *management system*, which a documented system can
  satisfy, rather than for a certificate ETABLIX does not hold. If that reading
  holds, the documented-not-certified position is adequate here and the
  coverage statement is the right attachment.
- **Code of Conduct, "Management System for environmental Protection"** — the
  same question on the environmental side.

The **Cyber Resilience Act** note is worth a look before the cybersecurity
module, and reinforces why Cyber Essentials is the cheapest thing on the
to-buy list that unblocks a named gate.

The **Responsible raw materials sourcing policy** and the **Quality Principles**
annexes are written for manufacturers and do not apply to a service provider,
but do not say so unprompted — answer them "not applicable, service provider"
only if asked.

## The one that would have failed the registration on its own

**The vendor legal name is JNN GLOBAL LTD, not ETABLIX.**

ETABLIX is the trading name. The registered company at Companies House is
**JNN GLOBAL LTD, company number 15405437**. A supplier registration whose
legal name does not match the register is bounced at entity verification
before a human reads a word of it, and that is the single most common reason
a good registration dies quietly.

So: legal name fields get **JNN GLOBAL LTD**. The trading name goes in the
additional information box and the USP, where it belongs.

**Local characters.** That field exists for suppliers whose legal name is
written in a non-Latin script — Chinese, Cyrillic, Arabic. A UK company puts
the identical name in both. Do not use it for the trading name.

**First and last name.** Enter these exactly as Companies House records the
director, not as you sign letters. The entity check compares them.

## Section 2 — the registration numbers

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| DUNS Number | *leave blank unless one is already held* |
| Tax number / VAT Number | *VAT number if registered; otherwise 15405437* |
| Legal form of company | **Other** → "Private company limited by shares (Ltd), registered in England and Wales" |

**DUNS.** If there is no DUNS number, leave it blank rather than inventing
anything. One is free from Dun & Bradstreet and takes days rather than weeks —
and because the Ariba qualification stage often wants it, applying for one this
week removes a future stoppage. Blank now is fine; wrong now is not.

**Tax number.** If the company is VAT registered, that is the VAT number
(GB followed by nine digits). If it is not, enter the company registration
number 15405437 and say "not VAT registered" in the additional information box
so the blank is explained rather than looking like an omission.

**Never enter the UTR here.** The Unique Taxpayer Reference is a confidential
HMRC reference, not a public tax identifier. It belongs on an HMRC return and
nowhere else, and certainly not in a supplier portal.

**Legal form.** None of the listed options is a UK private limited company.
"LLC" is an American form and a UK LLP is a different thing again; "GmbH" is
the German functional equivalent, which is close in meaning and wrong on its
face. Choose **Other** and type the real form. An accurate "other" is read as
precision; a wrong tick is read later as carelessness or worse.

## Section 3 — the financial fields

| Field | Answer |

| EBIT last year | *the true figure, or blank* |
| % of R&D invested from turnover | *leave blank* |

**Revenue and EBIT.** Enter what the accounts actually say, including a small
or nil figure, or leave them blank if the first accounting period is not yet
filed. These are checkable against Companies House in under a minute. An
inflated figure here is not optimism, it is the end of the relationship.

**R&D percentage.** Leave it blank. With a small turnover, any percentage is
either meaningless or flattering, and neither helps.

**Number of employees — the trap.** The lowest band offered is **11–50**.
JNN GLOBAL LTD has one working director and no employees; there is an
employer's liability exemption on that basis. Selecting 11–50 would be a false
statement on a form that also carries terms and conditions.

The field is not marked required, so **leave it blank** and state the true
position in the additional information box. If the form refuses to submit
without it, do not pick a band — say so in the additional information, submit,
and if it still blocks, write to the supplier support address and ask them to
record the true figure. A buyer who learns the real number from you is
untroubled by it. A buyer who finds it himself has found a misstatement.

**Business experience.** This asks about the vendor, not the director. The
company answer is **1–3 years**. The director's own record goes in the USP,
clearly attributed to him — which is both honest and more persuasive, because
it is specific.

## Section 5 — categorisation

**This section is read by a machine and then by a category buyer, and a wrong
tick here is worse than a blank one.** It does not merely fail to help: it
routes enquiries for work ETABLIX cannot do, which then have to be declined.
Declining a buyer's first enquiry is a worse first impression than never
appearing in their list.

The Level 2 list is a single run of options drawn from all three Level 1
buckets, and most of it describes components for power equipment. Read it as a
manufacturer's taxonomy, because that is what it is, and resist the entries
that merely sound right.

### Product Category (Level 1): **Direct** and **Indirect**

Not Logistic. Workforce transport is a package ETABLIX procures inside a wider
scope rather than a service it offers standalone, and claiming a third bucket
on the strength of one sub-package reads as unfocused.

### Product Category (Level 2): six ticks, and no more

**Direct**

- **Civil Works** — the compound: groundworks, hardstanding, drainage, access.
  Real, and the one category that puts ETABLIX in front of *project*
  procurement rather than indirect spend.

**Indirect**

- **Facility Management** — site services in operation: cleaning, catering,
  welfare servicing, accommodation management.
- **Waste Management** — a package held and controlled on every site.
- **Consulting** — Model 01 Advisory: strategy, specification, package
  boundaries, tender documents and evaluation.
- **Outsourcing Services** — the closest match in this taxonomy to Models 02
  and 03, where a whole service scope is managed under one accountable
  structure.

That is five. Add **Maintenance, repair, operations** only if you want enquiries
for the running phase of site services as well as its set-up; it is defensible
but it also attracts spares and consumables enquiries that are not ours.

### Two corrections to an earlier recommendation

An earlier draft of this sheet suggested **Containers & Ehouse** and
**Construction / Erection**. Both were wrong, and the reason matters.

**"Containers & Ehouse" is not site accommodation.** In a Siemens Energy
taxonomy an E-house is a prefabricated electrical building — containerised
switchgear and control equipment. Ticking it invites enquiries to supply
electrical housings, which ETABLIX does not do. Welfare cabins and
accommodation modules are not in this list at all, and that gap is handled in
the Additional information box rather than by borrowing somebody else's
category.

**"Construction / Erection" and "Installation/Erection" mean erecting their
plant.** Turbines, transformers, switchgear. ETABLIX does not erect plant, and
these entries sit among blades, bushings and bus bars for that reason.

**Also leave off, with the reason:**

- **Field Services** and **Field Service Labour** — Siemens Energy's own field
  service discipline, and the second is labour supply.
- **Temporary Labor** — the most tempting wrong answer on the page. It would
  classify ETABLIX as a labour supplier, the exact opposite of the position
  held everywhere else: defined packages, not labour-only. A category is hard
  to undo once a buyer's system holds it.
- **Engineering Services** — design and temporary-works engineering are bought
  in, not done in house. Ticking it invites a technical qualification that
  would have to be declined.
- **Building Services**, **Water Treatment**, **Fire Fighting** — permanent
  plant and building systems, not temporary site utilities.
- **Integrator** — conceptually close to "Management Integrator" and almost
  certainly meaning electrical systems integration here. The resemblance is
  linguistic, not commercial.
- **Real Estate Construction** — Siemens Energy's own premises, not a
  construction project's temporary village.

### UNSPSC

ones. Enter:

That segment is the right home for site establishment and site services. If the
field accepts more than one, add **80101600 — Project management** for the
advisory and control work.

**Confirm the eight digits in the UNSPSC browser before submitting.** The
segment is the part to rely on; the lower-level code should be checked rather
than taken on trust from this sheet.

### Siemens Energy Business Area: **Grid Technologies** and **Wind Power**

Both build large sites, often remote, with genuine site-establishment and
workforce-accommodation requirements — and Grid Technologies matches the
director's transmission background, so the conversation starts on familiar
ground.

**Gas Services** is a defensible third: a major outage is a temporary
population needing welfare, catering and sometimes accommodation at scale, on a
fixed and unforgiving date. Add it if you want outage work; leave it off if you
would rather be known for one thing first.

**Do not select all five, and do not select "Other".** A single-director
company claiming every business area reads as untargeted and routes to nobody
in particular. Two named areas reach two real buyers.


## Section 6 — Production capacity (required)

A required field with no honest numeric answer for a service business. Say so,
then give the figure that is actually meaningful — and keep it to one that will
be honoured.

> Service provider: no manufacturing or production capacity, and none is
> claimed. Delivery capacity on any project is the capacity of the specialist
> supply chain contracted for it, procured and scaled to the requirement rather
> than held as standing plant or headcount. ETABLIX's own capacity is the
> management capacity to specify, procure and control that supply chain:
> currently up to two concurrent engagements, at site establishments scoped for
> workforce populations in the hundreds and programme durations measured in
> years. Capacity is extended by contracting, not by recruitment, so lead time
> to mobilise a further engagement is the lead time of the packages themselves.

Set the "two concurrent engagements" figure to what will genuinely be honoured.
It is a commitment a buyer may hold you to, not a brochure line, and the
sentence after it is what makes a small number read as a model rather than a
limitation.

### What these bullets deliberately avoid

No certification is claimed, because none is held. No price, and no named
client, scheme or former employer. No turnover, headcount or growth figure
that the financial fields would contradict. Nothing implies a Principal
Contractor or Principal Designer appointment under CDM 2015 — the scope is
described as supply and control, not as a statutory duty holder. The
accommodation experience is the director's and is written as capability rather
than as a company track record, because the business-experience field already
says 1 to 3 years and the two answers have to agree.

## Section 9 — the fields to leave blank on purpose

**Reference customers — leave blank.** Nothing may be entered that cannot be
given if asked for. No former employer is named here under any circumstances,
and no client is named without their written consent. The additional
information box already explains the position properly.

**Existing contact at Siemens Energy — leave blank.** This is the most
dangerous field on the form.

There is no existing commercial contact at Siemens Energy. A connection on
LinkedIn is not one. Naming somebody who has not agreed to sponsor this
registration misrepresents the relationship to the buyer, and it puts that
person in the position of being asked about a supplier they never put forward —
which is how a goodwill contact becomes a closed door. A private message is
never a commercial lever.

Leave it empty. The registration stands on what it says.

## Section 10 — what to attach

Under **Add documents** and **Additional business related certificates**:

1. The company information letter — on letterhead, with the company number,
   registered office and the note on the common seal.
2. The ISO 9001 coverage statement — the clause-by-clause map, marked NOT
   CERTIFIED on its cover. Attaching this is a deliberate strength: it answers
   the certification question before it is asked, and it demonstrates a system
   rather than asserting a badge.
3. The health and safety policy, the occupational health policy and the
   anti-bribery policy.
4. A short capability profile covering the scope and the three engagement
   models.

**Attach nothing that claims a certification, an insurance or an accreditation
that is not currently held.** Every document in that list is true as it stands,
which is the only reason to send any of them.
