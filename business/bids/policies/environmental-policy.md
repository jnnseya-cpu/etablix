## 1. Statement

JNN GLOBAL LTD, trading as ETABLIX, provides construction management,
commercial and site-services consultancy. Its own direct environmental impact
is small — an office, business travel, and the energy and equipment behind
them. Its capacity to influence environmental outcomes is not small, because
the decisions it advises on determine what is procured, how much is wasted, and
how a site is run.

This policy therefore covers both: what the company does, and what the company
advises.

The company will comply with the environmental legislation applicable to its
activities, and with the environmental requirements of each client and each
site it works on, as a minimum rather than as a target.

**This company does not hold ISO 14001 certification and does not claim it.**
The arrangements below are the company's own, and are stated as such. An ISO
14001 roadmap sits in the company's compliance plan; until a certificate is
issued by an accredited body, nothing this company issues will suggest one
exists.

## 2. Legislation the company works to

The obligations that bear on this work, and which the company applies and
advises on:

- **Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 34** — the duty of care for
  waste: that waste is transferred only to an authorised person, is described
  accurately, and is accompanied by a transfer note.
- **Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011** — the waste hierarchy, applied
  in order: prevent, prepare for re-use, recycle, recover, and only then
  dispose.
- **Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005** — separation,
  consignment and the consignee return.
- **The Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) (England) Regulations 2001** —
  containment and bunding of stored oils.
- **Control of Pollution Act 1974, Part III, and the Environmental Protection
  Act 1990, Part III** — construction noise and statutory nuisance.
- **The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017 and the Wildlife
  and Countryside Act 1981** — protected species and habitats, and the survey
  and licensing that precedes work affecting them.
- **The Environment Act 2021** — including biodiversity net gain where it
  applies to the scheme.

Where a client's requirements exceed these, the client's requirements govern.

## 3. What the company does — its own operations

**Travel.** Site attendance requires travel and this is the company's largest
direct impact. Visits are planned so that one journey serves as many purposes
as possible. Remote attendance is used where it genuinely serves the purpose
and refused where it does not — a clerk of works inspection is not conducted
over video.

office; whether the electricity supply is on a renewable tariff; heating
controls. Say what is true, not what sounds good.]

**Paper and print.** Documents are issued electronically. Printing is by
exception and double-sided.

**Equipment.** Devices are kept for their working life rather than replaced on
a cycle, repaired where repair is viable, and disposed of through a licensed
waste electrical and electronic equipment route with evidence retained.

**Procurement for our own use.** Suppliers are asked about their environmental
arrangements as part of selection, and the answer is recorded.

## 4. What the company advises — where the real influence is

This is the part of the policy that matters commercially, and the company
expects to be held to it.

**Waste, before it exists.** The largest reduction available on any project is
the material that is never ordered. Where the company prices, procures or
manages site works it challenges quantity allowances, over-ordering and
specification that forces waste, and records the challenge whether or not it
is accepted.

**Site waste management.** Where the company manages site services, it
specifies segregation at source, licensed carriers, accurate waste descriptions
and retained transfer notes, and it checks that the notes exist rather than
assuming they do. Diversion from landfill is measured and reported, not
asserted.

**Welfare and accommodation.** Where the company specifies or manages welfare
facilities or worker accommodation, it specifies water and energy efficiency,
metering and controls, because occupied facilities consume for the whole
duration of a project.

**Pollution prevention.** Where the company inspects, it reports fuel storage
without containment, silt-laden discharge, uncontrolled dust and unmanaged
concrete washout as findings, and escalates in writing where they are not
addressed.

**Reporting what was found, including when it is unwelcome.** An environmental
finding is reported to the client as it stands. The company does not soften a
finding because the client would rather not have it.
