# Supplier Registration Service — "Describe your standard Selection Questionnaire"

Three fields: a name, a description, and CPV codes. It looks like
administration and it is not. This is the record buyers search to find you,
and the CPV codes decide which opportunities you are shown for.

## The thing to understand before you type

This is your **standard** Selection Questionnaire. You build it once and submit
it to many contracting authorities, not just to RM6242.

So **do not name it after RM6242.** If you do, you will either reuse a
questionnaire whose name no longer describes what you are applying for, or
rebuild the whole thing for the next opportunity. Name it after what the
company offers. The name should still make sense in two years.

---

## 1. Selection Questionnaire Name

> **ETABLIX — Construction Management, Employer's Agent and Site Services**

Specific enough that a buyer knows what they are looking at, general enough to
reuse. If the field is short, cut to:

> **ETABLIX — Construction Management and Site Services**

Put the trading name first. Buyers search on names they have seen.

## 2. Selection Questionnaire Description

> ETABLIX is the construction management and site services practice of JNN
> GLOBAL LTD. We act for clients, developers and contracting authorities on the
> management, commercial control and inspection of construction works: project
> management, employer's agent, clerk of works inspection, contract
> administration and CDM support.
>
> Our focus is the scope that sits around the permanent works — site
> preparation, site infrastructure, welfare, worker accommodation, site
> services and the subcontract packages that deliver them — together with the
> commercial control of the contracts that govern them.
>
> Services are delivered under one of three models: advisory, management
> integrator, or prime service contractor, selected against what the project
> needs rather than offered as a single fixed arrangement.
>
> We work across the United Kingdom. ETABLIX is a trading name of JNN GLOBAL
> LTD, registered in England and Wales, company number 15405437.

**Before you paste it, check three things.**

- Does it describe services you can deliver **today**? Remove anything that
  describes an intention. A description is read as a capability statement.
- Is prime service contractor genuinely on offer now? If not, cut that
  sentence. It changes what you are invited to, and see the CPV warning below.
- No prices, no rates, no fee percentages. Nothing on this screen is
  confidential to a buyer.

## 3. CPV codes — the field that actually matters

CPV codes are how buyers filter suppliers. Get them wrong in one direction and
you are never shown an opportunity. Get them wrong in the other and you are
shown work you cannot do, invited to price it, and have to decline — and
declining invitations damages the record that being admitted was meant to
build.

### The division-level trap

The list on the screen is the top level — every code ends `000000`. Those are
very wide nets.

**`45000000` Construction work advertises you as a contractor**, not as a
consultant. Select it and you will be shown works opportunities whose selection
stages ask for performance bonds, contractors' all-risks insurance and works
contract references — none of which the company holds today. Do not select it
unless prime service contracting is genuinely being offered now.

### Start here

**`71000000` Architectural, construction, engineering and inspection
services.** This is the division the whole offering sits in, and it is the one
that matters.

### Then use the search box and go narrower

A division-level code makes you findable but tells a buyer nothing. Use the
quick search to add the specific codes. These are the ones to search for, in
priority order:

| Search for | Covers |
|---|---|
| Construction management services | the core offering |
| Construction project management services | project manager workstream |
| Construction consultancy services | advisory |
| Construction supervision services | clerk of works |
| Construction-site supervision services | clerk of works, on site |
| Supervision of building work | clerk of works, building |
| Construction-related services | the wider group |
| Consultative engineering and construction services | technical advisory |
| Health and safety services | CDM support — select this if the CDM workstream is being offered |
| Quantity surveying services | commercial control, valuations |
| Contract administration services | employer's agent |
| Building and facilities management services | operate-phase services |

If site services are genuinely on offer, also search: **site preparation
work**, **refuse and recycling services**, and **cleaning services**. Search for
the accommodation and welfare terms rather than guessing a code — the portal's
own search is the authority on what exists.

**Confirm every code in the portal's search box before you select it.** The
codes above are named by their description rather than by number for exactly
this reason: the portal is the authoritative list, and a number typed from
somewhere else is a number that may not resolve.

### How many

Enough that a buyer searching for what you do finds you. Few enough that every
one describes work you could accept tomorrow. If you would decline an
invitation arriving under a code, that code should not be selected.

Somewhere between six and twelve specific codes, plus the division, is the
usual shape for a practice this size. Twenty-five is a supplier who ticked
everything, and buyers can tell.

---

## After this screen

The CPV selection is reusable and amendable. It is not a commitment and it is
not scored. What follows — the questionnaire itself — is scored, so do not
spend the afternoon on this screen. Set it, and move on to the Financial
Viability Risk Assessment and Attachment 3, which are the parts that take real
time.
