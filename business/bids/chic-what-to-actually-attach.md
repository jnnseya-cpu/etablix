# What actually goes in the upload when you do not hold the documents

The list in the last note was the full version. Most of it does not apply to
you yet, and the honest pack is much shorter. This is what to add.

## Start here: this stage does not ask for most of it

CHIC's route to participation is **self-certification**. Insurance, health and
safety and accreditation evidence is requested at ITT stage on a pass/fail
basis, and financial information at tender stage. That is confirmed in their
own guidance and it is recorded against the row in the register.

So do not hold the application back to buy insurance, chase an SSIP
assessment or file a set of accounts. None of those is the gate today. What is
being tested today is whether the company is real, whether the person is
capable, and whether you follow instructions.

The one rule that binds: **self-certify only what is true on the day you
submit.** A tick that is aspirational at RTP becomes a failed pass/fail check
at ITT, and by then it is a withdrawn application rather than a declined one.

## The line that decides what you can attach

There are two kinds of document, and they are not the same question.

**Documents you write.** A policy, a method statement, a CV, a forecast. You
author these. One written and dated this week is genuinely in place this week —
that is not fabrication, it is a company doing the thing a company does. Write
them, date them, sign them, and they are real.

**Documents a third party issues.** An insurance certificate, an SSIP
certificate, an ISO certificate, accounts filed at Companies House, a bank
facility letter. You cannot produce these and you must never imply you hold
one. If it is not issued, it does not exist.

Everything below sorts against that line.

## B. You can write these this week — and they count

None of these needs a third party. All of them are ordinary documents a
one-person company is expected to hold.

- **Health and safety policy and arrangements.** Under five employees there is
  no legal duty to have it in writing, but procurement expects it. HSE
  publishes a template for small businesses. Sign it and date it.
- **Equality and diversity policy.** One page.
- **Environmental policy.** One page.
- **Quality management arrangements.** Call it that. Do **not** call it
  ISO 9001 or format it to look like a certified system — you do not hold the
  certificate and the resemblance is the problem.
- **Method statement** for the workstreams you select, showing how work is
  planned, supervised, recorded and reported.
- **Named CV** for whoever delivers, which is you. Dates, employers, the scope
  you personally held, the interfaces you managed.
- **Statement of financial position** — the balance sheet as it stands, from
  the management accounts, however small.
- **Cash-flow forecast with assumptions written on its face.** Win rate,
  average engagement value, payment terms, cost base. A forecast with no stated
  basis reads as invention, and assessors read a great many of them.
- **Referees page.** Name, title, organisation, relationship to the work, and
  what they can speak to. **Ask each one before you write it down.**

## C. You cannot create these, and must not imply them

Professional indemnity, public liability and employers' liability
certificates. SSIP, SafeContractor or Acclaim. ISO 9001, 14001 or 45001.
Constructionline. Achilles UVDB. Accounts filed at Companies House. Any bank
facility letter.

For each one that is not held, the pack carries a single honest line rather
than a gap. One page headed **Compliance position**, and on it:

> Professional indemnity, public liability and employers' liability insurance:
> not currently in place. Quotations obtained from [broker]; cover will be
> bound before any contract is awarded and before any ITT submission that
> requires it.
>
> SSIP accreditation: not held. Application planned [month].
>
> ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001: not held and not claimed. Quality, environmental
> and health and safety arrangements are documented and attached at items
> [n]–[n].
>
> Filed accounts: JNN GLOBAL LTD was incorporated [month, year] and has not
> yet reached its first accounting reference date. Management accounts and a
> forecast are attached in their place.

That page does more for you than a thin pretence would. An assessor who reads
a clear statement of what is not yet held trusts the rest of the file. One
overstated claim and they trust none of it.

## The minimum pack worth uploading

If nothing else gets done this week, this is defensible on its own:

1. Cover and contents page
2. Certificate of incorporation
3. Your CV
4. Statement of financial position, and the forecast with its assumptions
5. Compliance position — the one page above
6. Referees, having asked them

Six items. Everything in section B that you get written before you submit
improves it; nothing in section C is worth delaying for.

## One thing not to do

Do not pad the file. A pack of eleven items where five are policies downloaded
and lightly renamed is weaker than six items that are yours. The assessor is
not counting pages. They are looking for the two or three things that tell
them whether the person behind the company has done this work before, and
your CV and your referees carry that on their own.
