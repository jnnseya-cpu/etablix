## Before you press send

**Fill in the name and the phone number.** An email asking to be considered as
a supplier, with a placeholder in the signature, answers the question about
your attention to detail before anyone reads the attachment.

**Send from contact@etablix.com, not a personal address.** It is the address in
the signature and on the website, and a mismatch is the first thing a
procurement function notices.

**Check the attachment actually attached.** Three point three megabytes goes
through a corporate mail system comfortably; the eight-megabyte original does
not, and an introduction whose attachment was stripped is an introduction that
never happened.

**Do not describe Hitachi's own contracting arrangement back to them.** Gary
told you how it works in a private message. The email says what ETABLIX offers
and lets the reader map it onto their own arrangements. Repeating his
description to his colleagues would expose him for a kindness.

## Afterwards

**Answer any SCM reply within one working day.** The speed of your first return
is the only performance data they have on you, and they read it as such whether
or not they say so.

**Never pay to register on a supplier portal.** A fee to be listed is not a
route to work.

**Declare only what you hold today.** "In progress, expected [month]" is a
respectable answer on a prequalification. A false one ends it, and verification
is routine in an SCM function.

**Come back to Gary once, whatever happens.** He spent a small amount of his own
standing on this. People who hear back give a second referral, and almost
nobody hears back.
