## Why only one question, and why this one

Fifteen of the sixteen lots are self-delivery works and are closed to this
company — no plant, no direct labour, no works contracting under the current
insurance. The resource-services lot is the only one shaped like what this
business actually sells, and SSIP is the single thing most likely to make it
pass/fail at selection rather than a scored criterion.

So the whole bid/no-bid decision turns on that one answer, and asking anything
else alongside it invites a longer, slower, more generic reply.

## What each answer means, decided before it arrives

| Answer | What we do |
|---|---|
| **Pass/fail, no equivalent, no commitment route** | Stop. The lot is not biddable this year. Buy CAS and return at the next framework. |
| **Pass/fail, but an equivalent is accepted** | Check what we hold against their list. Probably still stop, but confirm. |
| **Pass/fail, satisfied by a commitment before call-off** | Biddable. Prepare the bid and buy CAS in parallel. |
| **Scored, not pass/fail** | Biddable. We lose marks on that criterion and must win them elsewhere. |

Deciding this in advance is the point. A clarification answered while nobody has
agreed what it means produces a fortnight of argument about whether to bid.

## Download the questionnaire regardless of the answer

Even where the answer is "stop", the selection questionnaire is worth having.
It is a free, written specification of what every UK public framework will ask
this business next — and the gaps it exposes are the purchasing list for the
next twelve months. That has already been true of the CHIC return and the GCA
assessment.

## What must not be claimed in the bid, if there is one

- **No SSIP registration and no ISO certification are held.** Neither may be
  implied anywhere in a submission.
- **No employers' liability**, correctly: the company's only employee is the
  director, who holds a majority of the issued share capital, so it is exempt
  under the Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969. Write the
  exemption out in full rather than leaving the field blank.
- **PI and PL are in force at £5,000,000 each** from 26 October 2026, policy
  CHDB5802551XB. Check whether the framework requires each-and-every-claim
  rather than aggregate before stating the limit — that question is still open
  with Simply Business.
- The policy insures a **consultant**. It almost certainly does not respond to
  holding works contracts, which is a second reason the self-delivery lots are
  not available and the resource-services lot is.
