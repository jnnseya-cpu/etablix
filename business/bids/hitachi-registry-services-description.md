# Hitachi Energy supplier interest register — Main Products or Services

A registry field is not a sales paragraph. Three things happen to it:

1. **A buyer maps it onto a category** so the company appears in the right
   searches. So the words a Hitachi Energy buyer would actually type have to
   be in it — site establishment, welfare, temporary utilities, logistics,
   preliminaries, mobilisation, compound.
2. **Somebody reads it to decide whether to send an enquiry.** So the scope
   boundary has to be explicit, or the first enquiry is for something we do
   not do and the second one never comes.
3. **It is relied on later.** Nothing in it may claim a capability, an
   accreditation or a duty holder role the company does not hold.

Hence the "not provided" paragraph, which is unusual in a registry entry and
is the reason the rest of it can be believed.

---

## The full version — use this wherever the box allows it

> ETABLIX provides integrated site establishment services to contractors and
> developers on infrastructure and energy projects in the United Kingdom: the
> temporary systems a site runs on, from bid stage through mobilisation to
> close-out and reinstatement.
>
> Services:
>
> — Site establishment specification and preliminaries build-up at bid and
> pre-construction stage
> — Site systems diagnostic: the establishment requirement worked out from the
> programme, the layout and the labour catchment, with the interfaces and
> consents that gate it
> — Temporary compound arrangement and co-ordination: offices, welfare,
> catering, drying, storage and laydown
> — Temporary utilities: power generation and distribution, DNO connection
> enquiries, water, foul drainage and telecoms
> — Workforce logistics: accommodation strategy, travel and transport, site
> access, traffic management and deliveries
> — Site services procurement: package scoping, enquiry, normalised comparison
> and award recommendation for security, cleaning, waste, catering, cabin hire
> and small plant
> — Interface and consent tracking through mobilisation
> — Compound close-out, demobilisation and reinstatement
>
> ETABLIX acts as a single point of responsibility for site establishment in
> place of multiple separate subcontracts.
>
> Typical project types: substations, converter stations, grid connection and
> power quality compounds, overhead line construction, generation and storage
> sites.
>
> Not provided: permanent works design; Principal Contractor or Principal
> Designer duties under CDM 2015, which are accepted only by explicit written
> appointment and never by implication; and anything safety-critical requiring
> a competent person's certification, which is referred to that person rather
> than resolved by us.

## The short version — for a 400 to 600 character box

> Integrated site establishment for UK infrastructure and energy projects: the
> temporary systems a site runs on, specified at bid stage and delivered
> through mobilisation to reinstatement. Compound arrangement, welfare and
> accommodation, temporary power, water and foul, workforce logistics and
> traffic management, site services procurement (security, cleaning, waste,
> catering, cabin hire), and preliminaries build-up. A single point of
> responsibility in place of multiple separate subcontracts. Not permanent
> works design, and no CDM duty holder role except by written appointment.

## The one-liner — for a summary or a company strapline field

> Integrated site establishment — the temporary systems a site runs on,
> specified before the fence goes up.

## Notes on filling the rest of that form

**Category or commodity codes.** If it asks for UNSPSC, CPV or an internal
code, the honest fits are construction site preparation, temporary site
facilities and services, and construction project management support. Do not
claim an electrical installation or civil engineering contractor code — those
describe the work of the people ETABLIX specifies and procures, not what
ETABLIX does, and a buyer who sends a cabling enquiry on that basis gets a
"no" that costs the relationship more than the code was worth.

**Turnover and employees.** One working director. The figures are in the
management information pack, prepared by the director and unaudited, because
the company has not reached its first accounting reference date.

**Certifications.** None. Documented but uncertified arrangements exist for
quality, health and safety, environment, EDI and occupational health, and each
says on its own face that it is not certified.

**Geographic coverage.** United Kingdom. Say so plainly rather than claiming
Europe — the question comes up again in the Exyte thread and the answer has
to be the same both times.
