# "Two or three support themes" — 200 words

Three, each with a 90-day outcome somebody could check, and sequenced rather
than listed. The last two sentences say why they are in that order, which is
what separates a plan from a shopping basket.

**Pricing is the unusual one and it belongs.** Asking for help with pricing
sounds like an admission, and it is — but the specific version given here is
evidenced, self-aware, and exactly what commercial coaching on a scale-up
programme is for. A vague ask for "commercial strategy" would score nothing.

## Why these three

**Introductions first, because it is the same ask as the support opportunities
field.** Saying it twice, in the same words, is consistency rather than
repetition — and the 90-day outcome here is more concrete than the March one
there, so the two fields sharpen each other instead of overlapping.

**Compliance second, because it is the dependency.** Cyber Essentials is not a
certificate to collect, it is the thing that decides whether a client can hand
over a pack at all. Naming it as the second theme shows you know the
introduction is worthless until the data can move.

**Pricing third, and it is the one an assessor will remember.** Every applicant
asks for introductions. Almost none admits that the two models carrying their
revenue have no fee basis written down. That admission is evidenced — it comes
from the company's own pricing review — and it names precisely the moment it
will cost money: inventing a number under pressure in front of somebody who
negotiates weekly. A scale-up programme with commercial coaching can fix that in
an afternoon, which makes it the highest return per hour of anything on the
list.

**The closing sentence sequences them.** Opportunity, then capability to act,
then whether acting is worth it. Three themes with a dependency chain read as
someone who has thought about order; three themes in a list read as someone who
picked from the examples given.

## Before you paste it

nothing. The specific version is the whole value of the answer.
