## Do not reply with questions

She has answered everything asked, and she has told you where questions go:
**"If you have any specific clarification questions regarding the PSQ, you are
able to submit these via the portal."** That is not a suggestion. RM6242 is a
restricted procedure above threshold, so a clarification asked on the portal is
answered to every applicant, and one asked by email either goes unanswered or
puts the assessor in an awkward position. Asking by email after being told to
use the portal is itself a mark against you at a stage that tests whether you
follow a process.

A two-line thank-you is fine if you want to keep the contact warm. No
questions in it.

## The thing her reply actually changes

**A guarantor is not a precondition here.** Her words: complete the
questionnaires including the Financial Viability Risk Assessment, the finance
team assesses what you submit, and *"if there is then anything further that we
need, such as a guarantor or alternative financial assessment evidence, they
will come back to you and advise."*

So the order is: submit first, and let their finance team tell you what, if
anything, is missing. Do not spend a week chasing a guarantor before applying.
That was the right instinct on the other DPS because that assessor raised it
directly; here the buyer has said plainly it is a follow-up step, and only if
needed.

## The value bands are the lever — read this twice

Her first paragraph: you select the services you can provide **"as well as the
contract value bands that you can financially support."**

The Financial Viability Risk Assessment is proportionate to what you claim. A
company with no filed accounts that selects a high value band is assessed
against a high value band, and fails. The same company selecting the lowest
band it can genuinely support is assessed against that, and has a real chance.

**Select the lowest band you can support.** A DPS stays open for its whole
life, admits suppliers throughout and lets you amend what you are registered
for. Getting in at a low band and moving up on the strength of delivered work
is a route that exists. Failing the financial assessment at a high band is a
decline you then have to explain.

The same restraint applies to the service filters. Attachment 1 is the Filter
Matrix; tick only what you can evidence and deliver.

## The steps, in order

1. **Register on the Cabinet Office Supplier Registration Service** at
   `supplierregistration.cabinetoffice.gov.uk`. It is free. **Never pay to
   register on a public procurement portal** — if a page asks for a fee, you
   are on the wrong site.
2. **Find RM6242** and download the whole document set.
3. **Read `READ FIRST RM6242 DPS Needs v4.0`** before anything else. It is
   named that way for a reason.
4. **Open Attachment 6, the DPSQ.** This is the selection questionnaire
   itself, so it replaces guesswork about what is asked with the actual
   questions. Read it end to end before answering anything.
5. **Open Attachment 1, the Filter Matrix**, and decide the services and the
   value bands. Decide the bands before you start writing — every financial
   answer downstream is calibrated to them.
6. **Open Attachment 3, the Financial Assessment Template**, and complete it
   early. This is the pinch point for a company without filed accounts, and it
   is the part that takes longest. Get the accountant on it.
7. **Complete the Financial Viability Risk Assessment** with the management
   accounts, the cash position and the forecast — the same documents built for
   the other application. The forecast skeleton is at
   `business/bids/ETABLIX-cash-flow-forecast.xlsx`.
8. **Answer the DPSQ.** Parts 1 and 2 are company facts and exclusion grounds.
   Part 3 is selection. Where a question asks for something not held, say so
   plainly and say when it will be — self-certifying something untrue here is
   a ground for exclusion later, not a gap that gets forgiven.
9. **Any question you still have goes on the portal message board**, not to
   Jan Williams by email.
10. **Submit.** Then wait for the finance team rather than pre-empting them.

## What carries over from the CHIC pack

Almost all of it. The health and safety policy, the equality and diversity
policy, the environmental policy, the quality management arrangements, your
CV, the method statement and the forecast are the same documents. Build them
once. The differences are the questionnaire itself, the filter and band
selection, and the Financial Viability Risk Assessment, which is specific to
this route.

## One standing caution

Selecting a service element you cannot deliver gets you admitted to something
you then have to decline at mini-competition, and declining invitations damages
the record that admission was meant to build. Tick less than you are tempted
to.
