## ETABLIX — Site Establishment Requirements Report

### What it is

A written report that converts a request for "cabins, roads and utilities" into
a defined, phased operating requirement for one site — with quantities,
capacities, responsibilities and removal obligations that can be priced,
tendered and delivered.

It is produced for the party who will be accountable for the site: the
principal contractor, the developer, or the client letting the works. It is
advisory. **You contract every supplier. We never hold your supply-chain
money.**

### Why it is bought before it is needed

Site establishment is settled in the weeks before anyone is watching. It
becomes a preliminary allowance at tender, the allowance becomes the number,
and the number is defended for the rest of the job. By mobilisation the
decisions are made and the only question left is who pays for the ones that
were wrong.

This report exists to put a specification in front of that moment rather than
after it.

## What you receive

**Produced by us, as the deliverable:**

1. **Site constraints drawing** — boundaries, known services, exclusion zones,
   operational interfaces, and the areas we could not inspect.
2. **Findings and action register** — every observation with its location,
   evidence, information status, consequence, required action, owner and date.
   Observed, measured, reported, verified and unknown are kept distinct.
3. **Phased site establishment and logistics layouts** — one per construction
   phase, tested against the delivery and lifting sequence rather than drawn
   for convenience.
4. **Welfare and accommodation schedule** — sized against peak workforce and
   shift overlap, with walking distance, servicing, cleaning and sewage removal
   stated.
5. **Temporary utilities demand and connection plan** — connected load against
   realistic coincident demand, water flow and storage, foul capacity,
   lighting, communications, and the lead times that govern them.
6. **Temporary works register and design briefs** — haul roads, platforms,
   excavation support, hoardings, cabin foundations, each with design
   responsibility, loading assumptions and removal sequence identified.
7. **Survey and investigation schedule** — what to commission, to answer which
   decision, with coverage and exclusions stated, so you buy investigations
   rather than a standard package.
8. **Demobilisation and reinstatement requirements** — removal routes, access
   reservations and the obligations that must be in the supplier scope from the
   start.
9. **Photographic and baseline condition record** — roads, boundaries,
   neighbouring assets and retained structures, for use if damage is later
   disputed.
10. **A readiness statement** — what can proceed, what remains restricted, and
    what evidence releases each restricted activity.

**Specified by us, commissioned by you:** topographical survey, ground
investigation, utility detection and verification, drainage CCTV, structural
appraisal, asbestos refurbishment or demolition survey, contamination
investigation, ecology and arboriculture, swept-path analysis, baseline
monitoring. We write the scope and tell you what each must answer. We do not
carry out intrusive investigation.

## How it runs

| Stage | What happens | Working days |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Desk study** | We request and review your information, record each document's revision, coverage and limitations, and issue the list of decisions the visit must support | 5, from receipt of information |
| **2 · Site visit** | One day on site, in conditions that reveal the relevant problem. Attended by your construction lead and, on an operational site, by whoever controls isolations and permits | 1 |
| **3 · Draft report** | Full deliverable issued for your comment | 10, from the visit |
| **4 · Review and issue** | One round of comments incorporated; final issue | 5, from your comments |

**About four weeks elapsed**, subject to information arriving and the visit
being arranged. Where a second visit is justified — after rainfall, during a
delivery peak, at shift change — we will say so and agree it before it is made.

## What we need from you

Project scope and concept layout · ownership, lease and boundary information ·
planning permission and conditions · topographical and ground investigation
reports, where they exist · utility records and previous detection surveys ·
historic maps and demolition records · existing drawings and the health and
safety file · asbestos information · drainage and flood information ·
operational procedures and maintenance records, on a live site · site access,
induction and a named point of contact.

Where something does not exist, we record it as unknown and schedule the
investigation. **Missing information does not stop the report; it changes what
the report concludes.**

## What is not included

- Intrusive investigation of any kind, and no opening of chambers, entry to
  confined spaces, disturbance of materials or exposure of services.
- Permanent works design, structural design, or design of the asset.
- Any CDM 2015 appointment. **We are not the Principal Contractor and not the
  Principal Designer**, and nothing in this report creates or implies either.
- Procurement execution, supplier management or site delivery. Those are
  separate services under a separate appointment.
- Statutory applications, permits or consents, and any warranty that a
  permission will be granted.
- More than one round of comments at stage 4, or re-issue following a change to
  the scheme after final issue. Either is agreed separately.

## Limitations, stated rather than buried

This report is based on the information you provide, what is visible and
measurable on the day of the visit, and the published records we review. It
does not establish ground conditions, the position of buried services, the
condition of concealed structures or the presence of contamination. Each of
those requires investigation, and the report tells you which ones to commission
and what each must answer.

Areas that could not be inspected are recorded as such, and an inspection
limitation is reported rather than resolved by assumption.

The work is carried out to Great Britain practice, with England-specific
environmental guidance identified where it applies. Where a site sits elsewhere
in the United Kingdom or beyond, permissions and statutory duties require local
verification and we will say so.

Guidance is reissued. Where the report cites a statutory duty or published
guidance, it names the version current at the date of issue.
