# 04 · Existing logistics plan

Status: outline only, prepared by the EPC for planning submission.

Covered:
- Delivery routing from the strategic road network
- Abnormal load route and swept-path analysis for the 9 transformer moves
- Construction traffic management on the B-road, including a wheel-wash
  location and a passing place
- Predicted peak HGV movements: 45/day during civils

Not covered:
- Materials laydown zoning or storage allocation between packages
- Delivery booking or slot management
- Internal haul road construction, maintenance or dust suppression
- Waste segregation, storage or removal routing
- Personnel transport: no minibus/shuttle strategy despite a stated
  intention to limit car parking to 90 spaces against a peak of 280 people
- Plant movement and lifting coordination between packages
