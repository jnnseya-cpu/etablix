# 07 · Known mobilisation constraints

- Planning condition 14 restricts construction hours to 07:00–19:00
  Mon–Fri, 08:00–13:00 Sat. Two-shift working from Jan 2028 has not been
  reconciled with this condition.
- Planning condition 22 requires a Construction Environmental Management
  Plan approved before any site clearance.
- Ecology: badger sett within 40m of the Parcel B boundary. Licensed
  exclusion required; licence lead time quoted at 12–16 weeks.
- The access bellmouth requires a Section 278 agreement. Not yet applied
  for; local highway authority quotes 20 weeks from application.
- No mains water within 1.4 km. Nearest 11kV network connection point is
  800m from the site boundary; DNO quotation not requested.
- Ground conditions: made ground of unknown depth across the northern
  third of Parcel A. Two boreholes only, both in the permanent works area.
- Local accommodation: the nearest town has 180 hotel beds total and a
  competing infrastructure project 18 miles away mobilises in Q3 2027.
