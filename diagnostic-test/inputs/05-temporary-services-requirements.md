# 05 · Temporary-services requirements

Provided by the EPC as a one-page schedule:

- Site power: "adequate supply for offices, welfare and construction plant"
- Water: "mains connection to be arranged"
- Foul drainage: "connection or tankering as required"
- Lighting: "task and security lighting to the compound and working areas"
- Comms: "site-wide wi-fi and mobile coverage"
- Heating: "as required for winter working"

No loads calculated. No diversity factors. No resilience or standby
requirement stated. No metering or sub-metering strategy.
Accommodation services are not mentioned at all.
