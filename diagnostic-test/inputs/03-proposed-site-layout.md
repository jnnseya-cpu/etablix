# 03 · Proposed site layout

Total site 14.2 ha. Permanent works footprint 8.6 ha.
Land available for temporary works: 3.1 ha in two parcels —
- Parcel A: 2.2 ha north of the permanent works, adjacent to the access road
- Parcel B: 0.9 ha south-east, separated from Parcel A by the cable route
  easement, which cannot be built over or crossed by heavy plant

Proposed on Parcel A: main compound, offices, welfare, car parking,
laydown, materials storage.
Parcel B: currently marked "accommodation / overspill parking — TBC".

Access: single point off the B-road at the north-west corner. 6m wide
bellmouth as designed. No secondary access. Abnormal indivisible loads
(transformer deliveries, 9 movements) route through this point.

Car parking allowance shown: 90 spaces.
Site layout drawing is at concept stage only; no compound arrangement,
no drainage strategy, no laydown zoning.
