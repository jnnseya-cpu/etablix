# PROJECT NORTHREACH — KNOWN MOBILISATION CONSTRAINTS
**Marrowbridge Infrastructure Ltd** · Consents & Environment · 1 September 2026

---

## 1. Planning

Two authorities, two consents, two condition schedules. The full schedule
is at Annex A. The conditions that bear on mobilisation are:

**Ainsdale District Council** (converter station and northern route)
- Condition 8 — Construction Environmental Management Plan to be approved
  before any development. Officer advice: **8 weeks** to determination.
- Condition 11 — Construction Logistics Plan to be approved before any
  development. Submitted 14 May 2026, **not yet approved**.
- Condition 22 — hours of construction (see Annex A).
- Condition 27 — ecological mitigation to be implemented before clearance.

**Wetherfield Borough Council** (substation extension and southern route)
- Condition 5 — Construction Environmental Management Plan. Officer advice:
  **12 weeks** to determination. Not submitted.
- Condition 9 — archaeological written scheme of investigation.
- No condition restricting hours of construction.

## 2. Highways

A **Section 278 agreement** with Ainsdale DC is required for the new
bellmouth off the A6188 serving C1.

- Ainsdale DC quote **22 weeks** from a valid application to agreement.
- A **Stage 1 Road Safety Audit** must accompany the application. The audit
  takes **6 weeks** and requires a completed highway design.
- The highway design has not been commissioned (see the procurement note).
- No application has been made.

The A6188 carries a **7.5 t environmental weight limit** through Ainsdale
village. All HGV and abnormal load movements must approach from the north.

**Ryeford Bridge** (B4041, serving C5) requires a structural assessment
before any load above 18 t crosses. The bridge owner has quoted **26 weeks**
for assessment and any consequent strengthening design.

## 3. Ecology

- **Badgers**: an active sett is recorded in Parcel 7. A Natural England
  licence is required to close or exclude. Lead time **12–16 weeks**.
  Licences are not issued between 1 July and 30 November (breeding).
- **Nesting birds**: no vegetation clearance between 1 March and 31 August.
- **Great crested newts**: a district level licence is in place, purchased
  by NTP. Conditions apply to excavation within 250 m of the Marsh Lane
  drain.
- Ecological surveys for the badger licence application must be undertaken
  **between December and June**.

## 4. Utilities

- No DNO connection has been applied for at any compound.
- The water undertaker has offered a **standpipe agreement at C1 only**,
  capped at **8 m³ per day**, subject to a metered connection. Nothing is
  offered at C2–C5.
- Three third-party utility diversions (a 12" gas main, a BT duct route and
  an 11 kV overhead line) are programmed by NTP, not by Marrowbridge, and
  their dates are not in our programme.

## 5. Land

- Parcels 4 to 9 are held under option agreements with four landowners.
  Parcel 7 (Bracken Hill) includes a **25 m easement** either side of the
  existing 132 kV overhead line, within which the landowner's agreement and
  the line owner's consent are both required for any structure, storage or
  crane operation.
- Access to Parcel 9 crosses a third party's farm track. No agreement is in
  place.
