# PROJECT NORTHREACH — CONSTRUCTION PROGRAMME NARRATIVE
## Wetherfield to Ainsdale 400 kV Connection & Converter Station
**Marrowbridge Infrastructure Ltd** · Programme rev 4 · issued 22 August 2026
Prepared by: R. Vance, Planning Manager · Checked: not recorded

---

## 1. Scheme description

Design and construction of an 11.4 km underground 400 kV cable connection
between the existing Wetherfield 400 kV substation (extension works) and a
new converter station at Ainsdale, for Northern Transmission Partnership
(NTP) under a target-cost contract.

The route crosses two planning authorities: **Wetherfield Borough Council**
(southern 4.1 km, including the substation extension) and **Ainsdale
District Council** (northern 7.3 km, including the converter station).

Total programme duration **34 months** from access to practical completion.

## 3. Working hours and shift pattern

Single shift 07:00–18:00 Monday to Friday, 07:00–16:00 Saturday, for the
first thirteen months.

**From month 14 (May 2028), two-shift working is adopted across all
parcels** — 06:00–14:30 and 14:00–22:30 — to recover the float consumed by
the converter hall envelope sequence and to complete cable jointing within
the outage window agreed with NTP. Two-shift working continues to M09.

Night working (22:30–06:00) is not currently programmed but is held as a
recovery option for cable pulling only.

## 4. Float

The programme carries **four weeks of terminal float** against the
energisation date of 12 November 2029. NTP have confirmed the outage window
either side of energisation is fixed and cannot be moved within the
2029/30 outage year.

## 5. Resourcing assumption

Peak site population of **circa 300** is assumed for the purposes of this
programme, occurring during the overlap of converter hall fit-out and cable
jointing (approximately Q3 2028). Detailed workforce modelling is issued
separately by the Commercial team.

## 6. Access and possession

All five compounds are to be established under a single mobilisation
package. Access to C1 is on the critical path: the converter station
foundations cannot start without it, and the foundation sequence drives
M07.

Access to C1 is via a new bellmouth off the **A6188**, requiring a Section
278 agreement with Ainsdale District Council as highway authority.
Application status is with the Commercial team.

## 7. Assumptions and exclusions

- Assumes all planning conditions are discharged in time to suit the above.
- Assumes construction power is available at each compound from
  establishment. NTP have advised that the DNO connection is **not** in
  their scope.
- Assumes no seasonal restriction on the cable trench works.
- Excludes any allowance for archaeological watching brief beyond the
  four weeks stated in the Ainsdale conditions.
- Excludes abnormal load route works.
