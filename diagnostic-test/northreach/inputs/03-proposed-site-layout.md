# PROJECT NORTHREACH — PROPOSED SITE LAYOUT NARRATIVE
**Marrowbridge Infrastructure Ltd** · Temporary Works / Site Set-up · rev 1 · 3 July 2026
Note: prepared at tender stage. Not reissued following the two-shift decision.

---

## 1. Compounds

| Ref | Location | Area | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Ainsdale converter station site, off the A6188 | 3.2 ha | Main compound: offices, welfare, stores, laydown, batching |
| C2 | Wetherfield substation, within NTP's existing fence line | 0.9 ha | Substation extension works |
| C3 | Marsh Lane, Parcel 4 | 0.6 ha | Cable route satellite, southern half |
| C4 | Bracken Hill, Parcel 7 | 0.7 ha | Cable route satellite, northern half |
| C5 | Ryeford, Parcel 9 | 0.4 ha | Cable drum storage and pulling compound |

## 2. C1 main compound

- Two-storey modular office, 24 bays, for 96 staff positions.
- Welfare block sized for **220 operatives**.
- Canteen with 90 covers, servery, two sittings.
- Stores, 600 m² covered; external laydown 1.1 ha.
- Concrete batching plant (permanent works supply).
- **Car parking: 140 spaces**, plus 12 visitor and 6 accessible.
- Wheel-wash at the single vehicle entrance.
- Fuel storage: 2 × 10,000 l bunded tanks.

Access via a new bellmouth off the A6188, subject to the Section 278.
Single point of entry and exit for all vehicles including abnormal loads.

## 3. Satellite compounds C3, C4, C5

Each provides: site cabin (12 person), toilets, drying, small stores,
parking for 20 vehicles, and a materials hardstanding.

**C4 Bracken Hill** occupies the southern part of Parcel 7. The parcel is
crossed by an existing 132 kV overhead line running approximately
east–west. The proposed cabin cluster and the materials hardstanding sit on
the southern side of the parcel; the crane laydown for the cable drum
handling is on the northern side.

**C5 Ryeford** is reached from the B4041 over **Ryeford Bridge**, a
masonry arch over the Ryeford Beck. All cable drums (up to 68 t gross)
arrive by this route.

## 4. Ground

No ground investigation has been carried out at C1, C3, C4 or C5.
Hardstanding build-ups are assumed as 450 mm Type 1 over geogrid on all
compounds, taken from the Wetherfield project. Ground at C1 is described in
the site walkover note as "made ground of unknown depth over glacial till".

## 5. Constraints noted at tender

- C1 is 180 m from the nearest residential property (Ainsdale Grange Farm).
- C3 is within the flood zone of the Marsh Lane drain; the compound is
  raised 600 mm.
- C4 and C5 have no mains water and no foul connection.
- C5 has no mains power within 1.4 km.
- The A6188 has a 7.5 t environmental weight limit through Ainsdale
  village, 2.1 km south of C1.
