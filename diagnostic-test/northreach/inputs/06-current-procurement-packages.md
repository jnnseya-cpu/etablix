## 4. Delivery model — direction from the Board

The Board's direction of 28 August 2026 is that the temporary site
environment is to be let as **a single Prime contract with full risk
transfer to one supplier**, in order to remove the coordination burden from
the project team and give a fixed price at the earliest opportunity. The
project team have been asked to test the market for this.

Marrowbridge's expectation is a **fixed lump sum for the full 34 months**,
with the supplier carrying the risk of quantity, consent timing, ground
conditions and workforce fluctuation.

Indicative supply chain value of the temporary site environment, from the
tender build-up: **£14m–£24m**.
