# PROJECT NORTHREACH — TEMPORARY SERVICES REQUIREMENTS
**Marrowbridge Infrastructure Ltd** · Engineering · rev A · 19 February 2026
Issued to the supply chain as the basis of the temporary power and water enquiries.

---

## 1. Power

Estimated construction demand at C1, by area:

| Area | Estimated load |
|---|---|
| Site offices and welfare | 145 kW |
| Stores and workshops | 40 kW |
| Concrete batching plant | 180 kW |
| Tower crane (1 no.) | 95 kW |
| Site lighting (task and security) | 60 kW |
| Small power and welding | 120 kW |
| Converter hall temporary lighting and power | 210 kW |
| **Total connected** | **850 kW** |

Diversity has not been applied. The supply is to be sized by the successful
temporary power contractor.

Satellite compounds C3, C4 and C5: assume 25 kW each, generator supplied.

**Note:** NTP have confirmed there is no construction supply available from
the existing Wetherfield substation. A DNO connection at C1 has been raised
as an option but no application has been made. The base assumption in the
tender is **diesel generation for the full 34 months at all compounds**.

## 2. Water

| Use | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Welfare (drinking, washing, WC) | 0.28 l/s peak |
| Concrete batching | 0.55 l/s peak |
| Wheel wash (recirculating, top-up) | 0.09 l/s peak |
| Dust suppression | 0.15 l/s peak |
| **Total peak demand** | **1.07 l/s** |

Water at C1 is to be taken from the standpipe agreement being arranged by
the Commercial team with the water undertaker.

C3, C4 and C5: bowser supply, frequency to be determined.

## 3. Foul

Package treatment plant at C1, discharge to the Ainsdale Grange ditch
subject to a permit. Satellite compounds: cesspit, tankered.

## 4. Welfare

Welfare provision at C1 to be sized for **220 operatives** in accordance
with Schedule 2 of the CDM Regulations 2015. Satellite compounds: 12-person
cabins as per the layout narrative.

## 5. Comms

Fibre to C1 if available; 4G bonded router otherwise. C3, C4, C5: 4G.
CCTV at all compounds, recording to the C1 NVR.
