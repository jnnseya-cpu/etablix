## 3. Utilities in the vicinity — from the record search

- **Electricity**: 132 kV overhead line crossing Parcel 7 (see above);
  11 kV overhead line crossing Parcels 2 and 3, to be diverted by NTP;
  LV network terminates at Ainsdale Grange Farm, 180 m from C1.
- **Water**: 150 mm main in the A6188 verge, 340 m from the C1 boundary.
  Nothing within 1 km of C3, C4 or C5.
- **Gas**: 12" intermediate pressure main crossing Parcels 5 and 6.
- **Telecoms**: BT duct route in the A6188 verge; fibre availability at C1
  not confirmed.
- **Foul**: nearest public foul sewer is in Ainsdale village, 2.3 km from
  C1. No connection is practicable at any compound.

## 4. Site walkover note, 11 August 2026 — extract

"C1: level, previously agricultural, made ground of unknown depth over
glacial till along the eastern third where the former quarry access ran.
Standing water in the north-east corner after rain.

C4 Bracken Hill: the overhead line crosses the parcel closer to the
proposed cabin cluster than the layout drawing suggests. Recommend the line
profile is surveyed before the layout is fixed.

C5 Ryeford: access is over the masonry bridge. The bridge parapets are
close; a drum trailer will need an escort and may need parapet protection."
