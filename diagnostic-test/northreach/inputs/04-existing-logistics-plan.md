# PROJECT NORTHREACH — CONSTRUCTION LOGISTICS PLAN (DRAFT)
**Marrowbridge Infrastructure Ltd** · submitted to Ainsdale DC 14 May 2026 in support of condition 11
Status: **draft — not yet approved**. Wetherfield BC have not been sent a copy.

---

## 1. Scope of this plan

This plan covers vehicle routeing, delivery management and traffic
management for the **converter station site (C1) only**. The cable route
compounds are addressed by the separate Traffic Management Plan issued with
the street works applications.

## 2. Site personnel and travel

Personnel movements are based on a **peak of 280 site personnel**, of whom:
- 45% arrive by private car (single occupancy assumed)
- 25% arrive by car share (2.2 persons per vehicle)
- 20% arrive by site minibus from designated pick-up points
- 10% are resident within walking or cycling distance

Resulting peak car parking demand at C1: **158 spaces**.

Three 16-seat minibuses operate from Ainsdale village, Wetherfield town
centre and Ryeford Cross, each making two runs at shift start.

## 3. Deliveries

| Category | Vehicles per day (peak) |
|---|---|
| Concrete | 22 |
| Steel and cladding | 9 |
| Cable drums | 2 (campaign basis) |
| General materials | 18 |
| Waste and skips | 7 |
| Welfare servicing | 3 |
| **Total HGV movements** | **61 in / 61 out** |

All deliveries are booked through the Marrowbridge delivery management
system with 30-minute slots between 09:30 and 15:30, outside the school
peak on the A6188.

## 4. Abnormal loads

Eleven abnormal loads are anticipated: the converter transformers (4),
the GIS modules (5) and two site cranes. Routeing is via the A6188 from the
north, avoiding Ainsdale village.

Cable drums are **not** classified as abnormal loads in this plan.

## 5. What this plan does not cover

- Parking, access or deliveries at C2, C3, C4 or C5.
- Any vehicle movement associated with two-shift working. The plan assumes
  a single shift and a single arrival and departure peak.
- Movement of personnel between compounds during the working day.
- Accommodation-related travel (see the Commercial team's separate note).
- The Ryeford Bridge route.
