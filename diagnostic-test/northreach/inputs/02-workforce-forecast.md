## 4. Shift overlap

Under the two-shift pattern from month 14, the day and back shifts overlap
for **30 minutes at 14:00–14:30**. Both shifts are on site, in vehicles and
in welfare during that window.

The figures in section 2 are **shift-average**, not overlap. At the peak
quarter the overlap population is approximately **1.6 × the day shift**,
because the back shift is smaller than the day shift but not by half.

## 5. What is not included here

- No allowance for visiting NTP staff, ICP or DNO crews, or third-party
  utility diversions, which are managed by the client but arrive on our
  compounds.
- No allowance for the archaeological watching brief team.
- Driver and plant-operator numbers are inside the trade figures; separate
  logistics staffing is not modelled here.
