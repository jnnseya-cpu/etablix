# ANNEX A — PLANNING CONDITIONS BEARING ON CONSTRUCTION
Extracted from both decision notices. Ainsdale DC ref 24/0881/FUL (issued
9 January 2026). Wetherfield BC ref WBC/25/0442 (issued 3 March 2026).

---

## AINSDALE DISTRICT COUNCIL — 24/0881/FUL

**Condition 8 — Construction Environmental Management Plan**
No development shall commence until a CEMP has been submitted to and
approved in writing by the local planning authority.

**Condition 11 — Construction Logistics Plan**
No development shall commence until a CLP covering vehicle routeing,
delivery scheduling, wheel cleaning and parking for construction personnel
across **all compounds within the application boundary** has been approved.

**Condition 22 — Hours of construction**
No construction, deliveries, plant operation or vehicle movements shall take
place outside the hours of **07:00 to 19:00 Monday to Friday and 08:00 to
13:00 on Saturdays**, and at no time on Sundays or public holidays.

No exception is made for shift working. The applicant's Construction
Statement described a single day shift.

**Condition 27 — Ecological mitigation**
No vegetation clearance or ground disturbance shall take place until the
mitigation measures in the approved Ecological Mitigation Strategy have been
implemented, including any licensable works.

**Condition 31 — Lighting**
No external lighting shall be installed until a lighting scheme
demonstrating compliance with the bat mitigation strategy has been approved.
Lighting of the compound and haul routes falls within this condition.

**Condition 34 — Archaeology**
A programme of archaeological work in accordance with an approved written
scheme of investigation. Officer advice is that a **four-week evaluation**
of the C1 footprint is required before the compound is formed.

---

## WETHERFIELD BOROUGH COUNCIL — WBC/25/0442

**Condition 5 — Construction Environmental Management Plan**
As Ainsdale condition 8. Officer advice at pre-application was that
determination takes **twelve weeks**, not eight, owing to the consultee list.

**Condition 9 — Archaeology**
Written scheme of investigation to be agreed. No evaluation period stated.

**Condition 14 — Highway condition survey**
A condition survey of the B-road network within 1 km of the site access to
be agreed before development.

**No condition restricting hours of construction has been imposed.**
