**Rooms realistically available year-round within 30 minutes: about 90.**

Within 60 minutes the figure rises to approximately 310 rooms, but the
journey is 45–70 minutes each way on the A6188, which is single carriageway
for 18 miles and subject to seasonal tourist traffic.

## Observations

- The two local hotels report that the Wetherfield reinforcement project
  took 60 of their 74 rooms for eighteen months in 2022–23 and that rates
  rose by 40% over that period.
- The county tourist board records a summer occupancy of 91% across the
  district.
- No purpose-built workforce accommodation exists within the district.
- Ainsdale DC's local plan policy H7 resists change of use of dwellings to
  houses in multiple occupation.
