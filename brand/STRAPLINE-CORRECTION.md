# Logo strapline — the one correction, and why

**For the designer. Forwardable as it stands.**

Everything in the new lockup is right except the strapline. The device, the
ETABLIX wordmark, the "INTEGRATED SITE SERVICES" rule and the colours are all
correct and should not change.

## The change

> **From:** a Nseya Group Construction company
> **To:** a Groupe Nseya brand

## Why — three reasons, in the order they would cost us

**1. "a … company" asserts a parent that does not exist.** There is one legal
entity: **JNN GLOBAL LTD**, company number 15405437, registered in England and
Wales. There is no group, no holding company and no subsidiary. An endorsement
line tells a reader there is a parent, and the kind of reader who notices is a
procurement officer — who will search Companies House for the endorser, find
nothing, and stop. We already know this failure: three different forms of the
company name went to three different readers earlier this year, and the rule
that came out of it is that a name which does not resolve at Companies House is
where a serious reader stops.

The file is named `ETABLIX_Endorsed`, which is the brand-architecture term for
exactly this structure. The structure is the problem, not the typography.

**2. The word order is reversed from the registered office.** Our registered
office is **Groupe Nseya House**, Kingstanding, Birmingham B44 8DJ. The
strapline says *Nseya Group*. One lookup, two orders, and the reader cannot
tell which is the business and which is the building.

**3. "brand" is accurate where "company" is not.** ETABLIX is a trading name,
not a subsidiary. "a Groupe Nseya brand" is already the form on 54 files across
the website and the business documents, including the footer of every public
page. Changing the logo to say something different would make the logo
disagree with the page it sits on.

## What stays exactly as drawn

- The layered E device, both colours, and the two gold squares on the upright.
- The ETABLIX wordmark and its letterspacing.
- The gold rule and the "INTEGRATED SITE SERVICES" line.
- Transparent background, 3:1 proportion.

## What we need back

1. The corrected lockup, **as vector** — SVG or AI. The supplied WebP and PNG
   are lossy, so we cannot take brand hex values off them with any confidence,
   and a 380 KB raster is the wrong thing to put in a page header.
2. A **reverse version** for dark backgrounds. The site header sits on deep
   green and currently uses a reversed mark.
3. The two brand hex values as the designer intends them, so they can be set
   once in the stylesheet rather than sampled.

## If the group really is being renamed

If "Nseya Group Construction" is meant to become the actual group name rather
than a strapline, say so and stop — that is a different and much larger job. It
would mean the registered office building name, 54 files, the professional
indemnity certificate (which records *JNseya Construction Consultant*, singular,
no "and", no "s") and a Companies House check all have to move together, and
none of it should start from a logo.
